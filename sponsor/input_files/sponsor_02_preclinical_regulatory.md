# End-to-End Sponsor Playbook for Oncology Clinical Trials in 2026
## Chunk 2: Preclinical-to-IND/CTA and Early Regulatory Strategy

### Translational and Preclinical Package Design

In oncology, preclinical programs must support a credible hypothesis of clinical benefit, characterize on-target and off-target toxicity, and provide a scientific basis for starting dose and escalation strategy—while reflecting the life-threatening context in advanced malignancies. Sponsors often align nonclinical study planning to the oncology-specific framework in ICH S9, with additional studies staged as the program moves from refractory/advanced disease into earlier lines and potentially curative settings. [R17] [15]

A 2026 sponsor best practice is to generate a translational "line of sight" from mechanism → biomarker → patient selection → endpoint. For example, if ctDNA is part of early-stage solid tumor development, FDA issued guidance intended to help sponsors use ctDNA as a biomarker in early-stage (curative-intent) settings, including considerations for how ctDNA evidence will be evaluated in trials under IND and potentially for approval support. [R18] [16]

---

### IND/CTA Preparation and Activation Mechanics

For U.S. initiation, a sponsor submits an IND and generally must wait 30 days before initiating clinical trials; FDA reviews the IND during that time to assess whether subjects would be exposed to unreasonable risk. [R19] [17]

In the EU, sponsors operate under the Clinical Trials Regulation/CTIS framework: the Regulation enables a single online application via CTIS for multinational trials and has defined transition milestones (CTIS as single entry point for new submissions since 31 Jan 2023; full transition for ongoing trials by 31 Jan 2025). [R8] [18]

In the UK, sponsors running IM(P)-type trials must account for major regulatory updates taking effect in late April 2026, which can affect start-up planning, documentation, and contracting strategy for UK sites. Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency[19] and the Health Research Authority[20] have published implementation-oriented materials. [R20] [21]

### Early and Iterative Regulator Interaction

Because oncology programs commonly pursue accelerated pathways, sponsors benefit from structured, frequent interactions well before registrational commitment:

FDA provides a formal meeting framework for PDUFA products (with defined meeting types and expectations), and sponsors routinely use early interactions to align on endpoint acceptability, biomarker use, dose strategy, and design features like adaptations or master protocols. [R21] [22]

EMA scientific advice (and protocol assistance for orphan medicines) is a core platform for alignment on evidence requirements in Europe, and current procedural requirements (including timelines and process steps) are documented by EMA. [R22] [23]

PMDA offers consultations on clinical trials and regulatory submissions; sponsors often use these to reduce late-stage surprises when aligning global development plans to Japanese expectations. [R23] [24]

**Accelerated pathways and global alignment (2026 sponsor perspective).**

Sponsors typically build a "regulatory pathway map" (US/EU/Japan + selected other markets) and couple it to clinical evidence generation. In addition to U.S. expedited programs (Fast Track, Breakthrough Therapy, Accelerated Approval, Priority Review), sponsors may use EMA accelerated assessment (reducing CHMP review timeframe from up to 210 days to 150 days, not counting clock stops), EMA conditional marketing authorization (renewable annually with specific obligations), and Japan's SAKIGAKE designation system for certain innovative products. [R24][R25][R26] [25]

Sponsors increasingly also exploit "work-sharing" and parallel review constructs in oncology. FDA's Project Orbis provides a framework for concurrent submission and review of oncology products among international partners, and RTOR supports iterative review processes for certain oncology applications—both of which can influence how sponsors plan submission readiness and data packages. [R27][R28] [26]
