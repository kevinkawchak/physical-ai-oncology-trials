# Sponsor Organization for Oncology Clinical Trials in 2026
## Chunk 7: Sponsor-Owned vs CRO-Managed Activities and References

### Sponsor-Owned Versus CRO-Managed Activities

Sponsors may outsource execution, but sponsor accountability and oversight must remain demonstrable (21 CFR 312.52; ICH E6(R3)). [83] The table below shows a typical 2026 split in mid/large pharma oncology trials (hybrid outsourcing is most common).

| Lifecycle Area | Typically Sponsor-Owned (retained decision rights/accountability) | Commonly CRO-Managed (execution) | Sponsor Governance "Must-Haves" in 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery/IND-enabling | Target/product strategy; key nonclinical decisions; CMC strategy; IB ownership | Nonclinical study execution; specialized bioanalysis | Vendor qualification; controlled change; IB as essential record. [84] |
| Protocol & design | Final protocol approval; endpoint strategy; adaptive/master protocol governance; dose strategy | Drafting support; operationalization edits | Documented roles/responsibilities; QTLs/acceptable ranges; integrity controls. [85] |
| Regulatory interactions | Strategy; meeting leadership; final submissions | Submission publishing support; country start-up documentation | CTIS/portal capability; inspection-ready submission trail. [29] |
| Site selection & startup | Country/site strategy; investigator relationship governance; budget caps | Feasibility calls; contracts/budgets negotiation; document collection | Startup KPIs; escalation SLAs; eSystems controls. [86] |
| Enrollment & retention | Enrollment strategy; mitigation decisions (site adds, amendments); diversity plan governance | Patient recruitment operations; site management | Enrollment KRIs; protocol burden controls; decentralized oversight. [87] |
| Monitoring | Monitoring strategy (RBM design); central monitoring ownership (often sponsor-side) | CRA on-site/remote monitoring visits; report writing | Centralized monitoring analytics; CAPA governance; monitoring plan traceability. [88] |
| Safety / PV | Medical monitor accountability; safety signal decisions; DMC governance | Case processing (ICSR); vendor safety database operations | IND safety reporting compliance; DMC charter; data integrity controls. [89] |
| Data management & biostats | Final dataset approval; statistical analysis leadership; submission interpretation | EDC build; data cleaning operations; programming support | CDISC governance; conformance checks; validated systems. [90] |
| Quality assurance | Audit program ownership; inspection readiness leadership | Vendor audits support; TMF QC services | Essential records traceability; computerized system validation evidence. [58] |
| Closeout/archiving | Archive policy; regulator-facing archive accountability | TMF reconciliation; archival service execution | EU CTR: archive ≥25 years; sponsor-appointed archive responsible persons. [91] |
| Disclosure & publications | Disclosure governance; final narratives; risk/legal review | Posting execution support; lay summary drafting support | ClinicalTrials.gov + CTIS timelines; controlled approval workflow. [92] |
