This is the official template for the Master Thesis of the Master programs Artificial Intelligence and Computational Cognitive Science, but it is not mandatory to use this template. You are also free to use your own layout. \\

If you use it, don't forget to adjust the title page to reflect only your current study program (either AI or CCS). Also, check the name, appropriate titles, and affiliation of your supervisors.

As the field of AI is very broad, theses vary widely in their content. Each field of study requires their own specific sections. The sections mentioned in this template are the standard sections used in any scientific thesis and are meant as a starting point only. Discuss with your supervisor what sections and subsections are appropriate and necessary for your research. \\

References appear in the thesis like this \cite{mlp-universal} and also this \cite{rbf-universal}. The style of the references must correspond to what is customary in the specific scientific field.
