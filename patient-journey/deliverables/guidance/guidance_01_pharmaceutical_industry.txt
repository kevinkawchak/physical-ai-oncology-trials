==============================================================================
GUIDANCE 01: PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRY GUIDANCE
FOR PHYSICAL AI ONCOLOGY TRIALS
==============================================================================

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This guidance document provides recommendations for pharmaceutical sponsors
planning to integrate Physical AI systems into oncology clinical trials.
It covers trial design considerations, Physical AI system selection criteria,
regulatory submission requirements, data management with federated learning,
and a cost-benefit analysis framework.

This guidance applies to sponsors of investigational new drug (IND)
applications under 21 CFR Part 312 who intend to incorporate robotic-assisted
procedures, digital twin simulations, or federated learning infrastructure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TRIAL DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Physical AI systems should be integrated into the trial design during
    the protocol development phase, not retrofitted after trial initiation.

2.2 Sponsors should identify which trial phases will incorporate Physical AI
    and define the scope of AI involvement at each phase:
    - Phase I: Digital twin-based dose optimization and toxicity prediction
    - Phase II: Robotic-assisted biomarker collection and monitoring
    - Phase III: Full automated monitoring with federated data analysis

2.3 Adaptive trial designs are recommended to accommodate Physical AI system
    performance data as it accumulates during the trial.

2.4 Control arms should be designed to allow comparison of Physical AI-
    augmented outcomes against standard-of-care trial conduct.

2.5 Statistical analysis plans must account for any Physical AI-introduced
    variability in data collection or patient management.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PHYSICAL AI SYSTEM SELECTION CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Sponsors should evaluate Physical AI systems against the following:
    a. FDA clearance or approval status (510(k), De Novo, PMA as applicable)
    b. Compatibility with ROS 2 (Robot Operating System) middleware
    c. Digital twin platform interoperability and validation status
    d. Cybersecurity posture and compliance with FDA premarket guidance
    e. Clinical evidence of safety and effectiveness in relevant procedures
    f. Manufacturer support for GCP-compliant trial deployment

3.2 System qualification protocols must be established prior to first
    patient enrollment, including installation qualification (IQ),
    operational qualification (OQ), and performance qualification (PQ).

3.3 Sponsors should require vendor agreements covering system maintenance,
    software updates, and adverse event reporting during the trial period.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. REGULATORY SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS -- 21 CFR PART 312 SUBPART J
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 IND applications incorporating Physical AI systems should include:
    a. Description of the Physical AI system and its role in the trial
    b. System validation documentation and performance specifications
    c. Risk analysis addressing technology-specific hazards
    d. Cybersecurity plan per FDA premarket cybersecurity guidance
    e. Informed consent materials addressing Physical AI components

4.2 Under 21 CFR Part 312 Subpart J, treatment use protocols involving
    Physical AI must document that the system does not alter the risk-
    benefit profile of the investigational drug beyond acceptable limits.

4.3 Annual reports (21 CFR 312.33) should include a section on Physical AI
    system performance, including any technology-related adverse events,
    system downtime, and software updates deployed during the reporting
    period.

4.4 Sponsors should request a pre-IND meeting with FDA to discuss Physical
    AI integration and obtain feedback on the regulatory pathway.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA MANAGEMENT WITH FEDERATED LEARNING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Federated learning architectures enable multi-site model training
    without centralizing patient data, supporting privacy-preserving
    analytics compliant with HIPAA and GDPR.

5.2 Data management plans should specify:
    a. Local data processing and model training procedures at each site
    b. Model aggregation protocols and frequency
    c. Data quality controls applied before federated model updates
    d. Audit trail requirements for federated learning transactions
    e. Compliance with 21 CFR Part 11 for electronic records

5.3 Sponsors must validate that federated learning outputs are equivalent
    in quality to centralized analysis for regulatory submissions.

5.4 Differential privacy mechanisms should be applied to federated model
    updates to prevent re-identification of individual patient data.

5.5 Data governance committees should include representation from each
    participating site and the sponsor's data management organization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. COST-BENEFIT ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Sponsors should conduct a prospective cost-benefit analysis including:
    a. Upfront investment: Physical AI system procurement, installation,
       site qualification, staff training, and cybersecurity assessment
    b. Operational savings: Reduced monitoring visits, automated data
       capture, digital twin-based patient prescreening
    c. Timeline impact: Enrollment acceleration and safety signal detection
    d. Quality value: Reduced protocol deviations, improved data integrity

6.2 The analysis should model scenarios at minimum, expected, and maximum
    Physical AI adoption levels to bracket the range of financial outcomes.

6.3 Return on investment projections should be calculated per trial phase
    and for the overall program lifecycle.

6.4 Sensitivity analyses should address key uncertainties including system
    reliability, regulatory delays, and site adoption rates.
