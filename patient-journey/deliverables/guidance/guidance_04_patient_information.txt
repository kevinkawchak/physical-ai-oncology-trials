FOR PHYSICAL AI CLINICAL TRIALS

1. WHAT IS THIS DOCUMENT?

This information sheet explains how advanced technology called "Physical AI"
is used in the clinical trial you are considering joining. Please read this
document carefully. Your clinical team is available to answer any questions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS PHYSICAL AI?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Physical AI refers to computer systems that can interact with the physical
world. In clinical trials, Physical AI includes robotic systems that can
assist doctors during medical procedures, computer programs that create
virtual models of patients, and secure networks that allow hospitals to
share knowledge without sharing personal patient data.

These systems are designed to support your medical team, not replace them.
A qualified doctor is always in charge of your care. The Physical AI systems
serve as advanced tools that help your care team work more precisely and
monitor your health more closely.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW DO ROBOTS ASSIST IN SURGERY AND PROCEDURES?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In this trial, robotic systems may assist your doctor during certain
procedures. Here is what you should know:

- The robot does not operate independently. Your doctor controls and
  supervises the robot at all times.
- Robotic assistance can help your doctor perform procedures with greater
  precision and steadiness.
- The robot has built-in safety features, including automatic stops if
  anything unexpected occurs.
- Your doctor can take over manual control at any point during the procedure.
- The robotic system is tested and qualified before it is used with patients.

You have the right to ask questions about the robotic system before any
procedure. You also have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT IS A DIGITAL TWIN?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A "digital twin" is a computer model created specifically for you. Think of
it as a virtual version of relevant aspects of your health profile. Your
medical team uses this model to:

- Better understand how your body might respond to treatment
- Plan procedures more carefully before performing them
- Monitor your progress by comparing real results to predicted outcomes
- Adjust your care plan based on what the model suggests

The digital twin is built from your medical data, including test results,
imaging scans, and health history. It is a planning and monitoring tool
only. All treatment decisions are made by your medical team, not by the
computer model.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW IS YOUR DATA PROTECTED?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your privacy is a priority. This trial uses advanced methods to protect
your personal information:

Federated Learning: Instead of sending your data to a central location,
the computer systems at your hospital learn from your data locally. Only
general patterns (not your personal information) are shared with other
hospitals in the trial. Your individual data never leaves your hospital's
secure systems.

Differential Privacy: When general patterns are shared between hospitals,
additional mathematical protections are applied so that no individual
patient's information can be identified from the shared data.

Data Security: All systems use encryption to protect your data both when
it is stored and when it is transmitted. Access to your data is restricted
to authorized members of your care and research team.

6. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER FEDERAL REGULATIONS (21 CFR PART 50)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As a participant in this clinical trial, you have the following rights
under federal regulations:

- You must receive a clear explanation of the trial, including the purpose,
  procedures, risks, and potential benefits.
- You must be told about any alternative treatments available to you.
- Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may refuse to participate
  or withdraw at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you
  are otherwise entitled.
- You must be informed of any new findings during the trial that may affect
  your willingness to continue participating.
- You have the right to ask questions about the trial at any time.
- Your confidentiality will be maintained to the extent permitted by law.
- You will receive a copy of the signed consent form for your records.
