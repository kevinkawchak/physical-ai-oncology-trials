==============================================================================
GUIDANCE 02: FIELD OBSERVER GUIDE
FOR PHYSICAL AI CLINICAL TRIAL SITES
==============================================================================

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This guidance document defines the role, responsibilities, and procedures
for field observers assigned to clinical trial sites deploying Physical AI
systems in oncology trials. Field observers serve as independent monitors
of robot-assisted procedures, safety events, and data integrity at the
point of care.

This guidance is applicable to all personnel designated as field observers
under the trial monitoring plan, operating under ICH E6(R2) Good Clinical
Practice and 21 CFR Part 312 requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ROLE OF THE FIELD OBSERVER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 The field observer is an independent, trained individual responsible for
    on-site observation of Physical AI system operations during clinical
    trial activities.

2.2 Primary responsibilities include:
    a. Observing robot-assisted clinical procedures in real time
    b. Documenting Physical AI system performance and any anomalies
    c. Monitoring patient safety during and after robot interactions
    d. Verifying that Physical AI operations conform to the trial protocol
    e. Reporting safety events and technology-related incidents
    f. Serving as a liaison between the site, sponsor, and data safety
       monitoring board (DSMB) for Physical AI-specific observations

2.3 The field observer does not direct clinical care, operate Physical AI
    systems, or make clinical decisions. Their role is observational and
    documentary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT TO OBSERVE DURING ROBOT-ASSISTED PROCEDURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Pre-Procedure Observations:
    a. Confirm system startup and self-check completion
    b. Verify calibration status of robotic instruments
    c. Confirm patient identity verification by the clinical team
    d. Observe informed consent confirmation for Physical AI procedures
    e. Document environmental conditions (room setup, equipment placement)

3.2 Intra-Procedure Observations:
    a. Robot movement accuracy and smoothness
    b. Response time of the system to operator commands
    c. Any unexpected system behaviors (pauses, alerts, error messages)
    d. Patient physiological response during robot interaction
    e. Communication between the clinical team and the system interface
    f. Any manual overrides of automated functions

3.3 Post-Procedure Observations:
    a. System shutdown and data upload confirmation
    b. Patient condition assessment following robot interaction
    c. Clinical team debriefing notes regarding system performance
    d. Any system maintenance or recalibration activities

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAFETY MONITORING CHECKLIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The field observer must complete the following checklist for each observed
procedure. All items require documented yes/no/NA responses with comments.

    [ ] Physical AI system passed pre-procedure self-check
    [ ] Emergency stop mechanisms verified as functional
    [ ] Clinical team confirmed readiness for robot-assisted procedure
    [ ] Patient monitoring equipment active and displaying correctly
    [ ] No unexpected system alerts during procedure
    [ ] No unplanned manual overrides required
    [ ] Patient vital signs remained within acceptable ranges
    [ ] No unanticipated contact between robot and patient or staff
    [ ] System data successfully uploaded to trial database
    [ ] Post-procedure patient assessment completed by clinical team

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ADVERSE EVENT RECOGNITION SPECIFIC TO PHYSICAL AI
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Technology-related adverse events include but are not limited to:
    a. Unintended robot movement causing patient contact or injury
    b. System failure during an active clinical procedure
    c. Incorrect data capture leading to erroneous clinical decisions
    d. Cybersecurity breach affecting patient data or system operation
    e. Digital twin model output that deviates significantly from actual
       patient response, leading to inappropriate clinical action
    f. Communication failure between robotic system and MCP server

5.2 Severity classification for Physical AI adverse events:
    - Minor: System anomaly with no patient impact, resolved by restart
    - Moderate: System event requiring manual intervention, no patient harm
    - Serious: System event resulting in patient harm or potential harm
    - Critical: System event resulting in serious injury or death

5.3 All adverse events must be reported within 24 hours to the sponsor and
    within the timeframes specified in 21 CFR 312.32 for serious adverse
    events. Technology-related events must also be reported to the
    Physical AI system manufacturer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Field observers must maintain the following documentation:
    a. Observation logs for each witnessed procedure (date, time, duration)
    b. Completed safety monitoring checklists
    c. Adverse event reports with Physical AI-specific detail
    d. Photographic or video documentation where permitted by protocol
    e. Field notes on system performance trends across multiple procedures
    f. Communication logs with the sponsor and DSMB

6.2 All documentation must comply with 21 CFR Part 11 requirements for
    electronic records where applicable.

6.3 Source documents must be retained for the duration specified in the
    trial protocol and in accordance with 21 CFR 312.62.

6.4 Field observers should submit weekly summary reports to the sponsor's
    clinical operations team and flag any emerging safety patterns.
