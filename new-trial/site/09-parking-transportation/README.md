# Physical AI Trial Site Parking and Patient Transportation Standards

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Parking facility design, patient access, and transportation demand management
standards for Physical AI oncology clinical trial sites. Covers a 120-space
new-construction parking facility with 24-hour operations, EV charging,
accessible parking, transit integration, and sustainability requirements.

## Key Provisions

- 120 parking spaces (80 patient, 15 staff, 10 accessible, 5 drop-off, 10 ride-share)
- Covered patient drop-off zone for 3 vehicles with 20-foot canopy
- Transit integration within 1/4 mile of BART/Muni
- 20% EV Level 2 charging, 4 DC fast charging stations
- 24-hour monitoring with emergency call stations
- 30 bicycle parking spaces with e-bike charging
- Emergency vehicle access with dedicated ambulance bay
- Solar or green roof on 50% of parking structure rooftop
- Stormwater management per SF Green Infrastructure Ordinance
