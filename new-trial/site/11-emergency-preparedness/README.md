# Physical AI Trial Site Emergency Preparedness Plan

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Comprehensive emergency preparedness covering robot system failures,
cybersecurity incidents, natural disasters, and clinical emergencies for
Physical AI oncology clinical trial sites. Includes four-level emergency
classification, Physical AI-specific response procedures, business continuity,
and quarterly drill requirements.

## Key Provisions

- Four-level emergency classification system (Minor to Catastrophic)
- Physical AI emergency stop protocol with system-specific behaviors
- Cybersecurity incident response for 5 Physical AI threat categories
- Seismic preparedness with automated safe-mode triggers
- Power failure response (dual feeds, UPS, 72-hour generators)
- Clinical emergency procedures during Physical AI procedures
- Business continuity with mutual aid agreements and remote backup
- Quarterly drill rotation (system failure, cyber, evacuation, power)
