# AB 2847 - California Physical AI Patient Rights and Robotic Safety Act

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Assembly Bill 2847 establishes a comprehensive Patient Bill of Rights for
Physical AI oncology clinical trials and robotic safety certification standards.
Control has shifted toward the patient's side, with expanded rights including
on-demand 24/7 scheduling, full disclosure, human intervention, data control,
equitable access, and pediatric protections.

## Key Provisions

- Patient Bill of Rights with six categories of protected rights
- Right to on-demand scheduling (24/7, average 8-minute wait times)
- Right to full disclosure of robot types, USL scores, and safety records
- Right to human intervention at any point during procedures
- Right to privacy and data control including digital twin deletion
- Pediatric patient protections aligned with adapted 21 CFR Part 50
- Robotic safety certification and continuous monitoring requirements
- Patient complaint and grievance process

## Evidence Base

The simulation demonstrated 168 patients with 4.7/5.0 satisfaction, seven
adverse events all resolved same-hour, and 99.7% fleet uptime. Patient-facing
instructions validated for all 10 robot types.
