# FDA Physical AI Oncology Trial Site National Compliance Guide

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Federal regulatory compliance guide identifying where corrections and additions
should be applied to existing regulations for Physical AI oncology clinical
trial sites. Covers 21 CFR Parts 50, 312, 11, 820, HIPAA, and NRC 10 CFR
Part 35, with cross-references to three completed regulatory adaptations.

## Key Provisions

- Section-by-section correction guide for 21 CFR Part 50 (5 sections)
- Section-by-section correction guide for 21 CFR Part 312 (10 sections)
- ICH E6(R3) adaptation guide (7 areas)
- 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records updates for Physical AI telemetry
- 21 CFR Part 820 quality system updates for Physical AI devices
- HIPAA compliance for robotic telemetry and digital twin PHI
- NRC compliance for radiotherapy Physical AI systems
- Cross-reference matrix mapping 10 robot types to applicable regulations
