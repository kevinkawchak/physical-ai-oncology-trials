# California Code of Regulations - Physical AI Trial Site Regulations

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Proposed Title 22, Division 5, Chapter 14 regulations implementing the
California Physical AI Oncology Clinical Trial Authorization Act (SB 1042).
Establishes statewide standards for site authorization, Physical AI system
registration, staffing, training, inspection, enforcement, and reporting.

## Key Provisions

- Authorization process with 120-day review timeline
- Physical AI system registration with USL/PSL scoring requirements
- Minimum USL thresholds by system category (3.0 to 7.0)
- Maintenance and calibration coordination standards
- Minimum staffing: 8-10 FTE across three shifts with on-call specialists
- 160-hour site safety officer training and certification program
- Quarterly unannounced inspections
- Monthly and annual reporting requirements
- Enforcement actions from deficiency notices to authorization revocation
