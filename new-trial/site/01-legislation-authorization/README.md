# SB 1042 - California Physical AI Oncology Clinical Trial Authorization Act

**Version**: 2.9.0
**Last Updated**: March 2026

## Overview

Senate Bill 1042 establishes the legal framework for authorizing Physical AI
oncology clinical trial sites in California. This legislation creates a
standardized authorization process through the California Department of Public
Health, defines Physical AI system categories and scoring requirements, and
establishes safety, reporting, and workforce transition provisions.

## Key Provisions

- Authorization process for Physical AI oncology clinical trial sites
- Definitions for 10 Physical AI system categories based on the simulation
- USL and PSL scoring requirements for site and system compliance
- Phase 0 simulation validation requirements
- Informed consent and patient rights provisions
- Adverse event reporting aligned with adapted 21 CFR Part 312
- Workforce transition programs
- Relationship to existing California AI legislation (AB 489, AB 3030, SB 1120, SB 243, AB 2013)

## Evidence Base

Based on the 24-hour on-demand Physical AI oncology clinical trial simulation
(March 23, 2026) demonstrating 168 patients across 15 cancer types served by
29 robot instances with 99.7% facility uptime and zero patient harm.
