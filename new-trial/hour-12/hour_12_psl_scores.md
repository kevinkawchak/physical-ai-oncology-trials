PSL and USL measure different aspects: USL focuses on technical unification
readiness while PSL focuses on clinical trial performance (omniscience,
omnipresence, omnipotence). The Rehab Exoskeleton PSL increase this hour
reflects clinical gait training outcomes rather than technical interoperability
improvements, illustrating the distinction between the two frameworks.

The single-patient cancer journey framework (Kawchak, 2026;
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19119939) demonstrated autonomous Physical AI trial
orchestration for an individual patient. Hour 12 PSL scoring extends this
evaluation to multi-patient concurrent operations with 10 robot types serving
approximately 25 patients across 11 cancer types simultaneously.
