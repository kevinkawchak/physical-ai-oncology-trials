### AE-19-001: Anxiety Response with Tachycardia - PAT-ODMND-0158

- Patient: PAT-ODMND-0158 (63F, brain metastases, Stage IV, ECOG 1)
- Time of onset: 19:42
- Setting: RT Positioning, Vault 1, RTPOS-01 active
- Event: During thermoplastic mask application for SRS positioning, patient
  developed acute anxiety response with tachycardia. Heart rate elevated
  from baseline 76 bpm to 112 bpm. Patient reported claustrophobia and
  difficulty breathing. No desaturation (SpO2 maintained at 96%).
- CTCAE Grade: 1 (mild; transient anxiety, HR elevation without
  hemodynamic compromise)
- Immediate actions:
  - 19:42 - RTPOS-01 paused positioning sequence (safety interlock)
  - 19:42 - Thermoplastic mask loosened by radiation therapist
  - 19:43 - Patient reassured, guided breathing initiated
  - 19:43 - Vital signs monitored continuously (1-min intervals)
  - 19:44 - HR 102, BP 148/88, SpO2 96%, RR 20
  - 19:45 - HR 94, BP 142/84, SpO2 97%, RR 18
  - 19:46 - HR 88, BP 138/82, SpO2 97%, RR 16
  - 19:47 - HR 82, BP 134/80, SpO2 97%, RR 15 (normalized)
  - 19:47 - 5-minute break completed. Patient confirms readiness to resume.
  - 19:48 - Mask re-applied with modified fit (additional ventilation holes)
  - 19:48 - RTPOS-01 positioning resumed
  - 19:58 - Positioning completed successfully
- Causality assessment: Procedure-related (claustrophobic response to mask).
  Not related to robot malfunction. RTPOS-01 safety interlock functioned as
  designed, pausing all motion within 200 ms of therapist trigger.
- Reporting: Documented per ICH E6(R3) Section 2.10 (adverse event
  recording and reporting in clinical trials). Entered in site adverse
  event log. Grade 1 event does not meet expedited reporting threshold
  per 21 CFR 312.32(c) but recorded in case report form per 21 CFR 312.62.
- Follow-up: Patient tolerated remainder of positioning session. Note added
  to patient record for future sessions recommending pre-procedure anxiolytic
  and modified mask with enhanced ventilation.
