The Surgical Robots Dim A increase (7.3 to 7.4) narrows the gap between the
PSL omniscient score and the USL technical interoperability benchmark for the
da Vinci platform. Multi-surgery day data aggregation provides a rich
training corpus for surgical AI models, directly supporting the omniscient
dimension's emphasis on knowledge depth and breadth. The Cobots Dim B increase
(6.6 to 6.7) reflects operational fleet management improvements that enhance
the omnipresent scoring relative to the Franka Panda's USL benchmark, where
multi-station availability is a key differentiator for collaborative robot
deployment in clinical environments.

PSL and USL measure different aspects: USL focuses on technical unification
readiness while PSL focuses on clinical trial performance (omniscience,
omnipresence, omnipotence). The correlation between USL and PSL is moderate,
as high technical interoperability does not automatically translate to high
clinical omniscience or omnipresence.
