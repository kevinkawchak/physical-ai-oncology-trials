In a recent 24-Hour on-demand Physical AI oncology trial simulation, hourly results of patients and robots were updated throughout the project with high proficiency. I demoed these Claude Code outputs to two audiences, with favorable feedback.

This patient-centric single site simulation with 1 minute resolution (1,440 total minutes):
1) Demonstrated how 29 robot instances served 175 unique patients across 15 cancer types autonomously with minimal human staff. 
2) In total, 185 robotic procedures were performed, with 7 adverse events (all were resolved) in less than 2 hours of AI processing.

24 Directories (1 Per Hour):
1) Each directory contains 3 text diagrams regarding facility, patient, and robot current status.
2) Each directory: 4 files of patient records, standard scoring, robot logs, and the simulation.
3) Each directory: Builds upon previous hour’s results with comprehensive patient vitals.

25th Directory (Final Update): 
1) Patient volume summary, procedures by type, drug administration summary, and patient wait times. Attached: Final diagram patient flow.
