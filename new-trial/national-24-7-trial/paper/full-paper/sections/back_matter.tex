\section*{Ethical Disclosures}
\label{sec:ethics}

The author of the article declares no competing interests. None of
the four simulations described in this paper involved real patients,
real protected health information, or real institutional review board
oversight. All patient identifiers, vital signs, robot logs, agent
decisions, and outcomes are synthetic or illustrative. The work is
independent and is not endorsed or sponsored by any trial sponsor,
FDA, CRO, site, IRB, regulator, or medical society; and was generated
using Artificial Intelligence.
