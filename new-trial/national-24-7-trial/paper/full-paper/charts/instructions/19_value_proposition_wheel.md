## Value Proposition Wedges

1. Cross-modal: "Reasons across protocol, EHR, robot telemetry, regulatory
   adaptations, and ASCII diagrams in a single inference pass."
2. Hourly cadence: "Emits one commit per hour; supervised models release
   per cohort or per visit."
3. Repository scale: "Holds the entire trial repository in context; narrow
   models hold only the training feature set."
4. Multi-perspective: "Operates as site coordinator, patient orchestrator,
   or sponsor decision agent."
5. Local-friendly: "Local re-run on Core i5-6200U with 4 GB RAM;
   supervised models often need GPU clusters."
6. Auditable trail: "GitHub commit log per hour; supervised models
   typically release a single trained-weight artifact."
