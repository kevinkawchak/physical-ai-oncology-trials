## Document Overview: `FDA_RealTime_Clinical_Trials.md`

This file is a faithful Markdown conversion of the official FDA News Release titled **"FDA Announces Major Steps to Implement Real-Time Clinical Trials"**, published **April 28, 2026**.

### Content Summary

The document covers:
1. **Context** — Why early-phase clinical trials are a bottleneck in drug development and how AI/data science can address this.
2. **Proof-of-Concept Trials** — Two active RTCT trials initiated by AstraZeneca (TRAVERSE, Phase 2, mantle cell lymphoma) and Amgen (STREAM-SCLC, Phase 1b, small cell lung carcinoma), with Paradigm Health facilitating real-time signal delivery to the FDA.
3. **Pilot Program RFI** — A Request for Information soliciting public input on the design, metrics, and success criteria for a broader RTCT pilot program.
4. **Continuous Trials Vision** — The FDA's long-term goal of eliminating inter-phase development gaps through continuous, real-time trial monitoring.
5. **Timeline** — Key dates: RFI closes May 29, 2026; final criteria July 2026; pilot selections August 2026.
6. **Quotes** — Attributed statements from FDA Commissioner Marty Makary, M.D., M.P.H. and Chief AI Officer Jeremy Walsh.
