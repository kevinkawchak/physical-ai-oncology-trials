\subsection{Study Discontinuation and Closure}
\draftinstr{Closure by the Sponsor-Investigator, single IRB, or FDA; the Physical AI
clinical-hold grounds (hard cyber-physical limit breach, verification-before-generation
gate-surface failure, loss of audit integrity) that may halt one site or the fleet;
and the controlled decommissioning and data-preservation procedure (\S10.10).}

\subsection{Confidentiality and Privacy}
\draftinstr{HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification (18 identifiers), deny-by-default
authorization, and the federated hash-chained audit trail under 21 CFR part 11
\cite{ecfr2026part11}. (The audit-trail data flow is the Figure 21 capital-firewall
companion logic; the hash-chained loop itself is rendered in Figure 11, \S6.)}

\subsection{Future Use of Stored Specimens and Data}
\draftinstr{The tiered telemetry pyramid (apex summaries and safety / audit logs;
middle compressed feature streams; L0 raw losslessly reconstructable), L0 deposited
on Zenodo and decompressible on FDA request per 21 CFR \S312.57; ctDNA and biomarker
specimens governed by consent.}

\subsection{Key Roles and Study Governance}
\draftinstr{Sponsor-Investigator (ChemicalQDevice, Kevin Kawchak) holding the
combined IND/IDE under parts 312 and 812; Coordinating Center; site PIs; the Arm A
operating surgeon, primary operator, and backup operator under supervised
semiautonomy; the CRO; and the DSMB / ISM / Physical AI Safety Review Committee, on a
delegation-of-authority log.}

\subsection{Safety Oversight}
\draftinstr{Three tiers: the DSMB (group-sequential interim with O'Brien-Fleming
spending \cite{DeMets1994lan} and the \S9 stopping rules), the per-procedure
Independent Safety Monitor, and the Physical AI Safety Review Committee ($\leq 90$-day
cadence) reviewing the fleet telemetry, gate-surface decisions, and USL rating.}

\subsection{Clinical Monitoring}
\draftinstr{Robotics- and AI-competent CRO monitors; risk-based plan; source-data
verification of clinical documents, consent (including the Arm A Physical AI opt-out
election), and the cyber-physical evidence (telemetry, gate-surface logs, federated
audit trail).}

\subsection{Quality Assurance and Quality Control}
\draftinstr{Pre-procedure safety-matrix verification and USL confirmation before
every Arm A case; Phase 0 re-validation, USL reassessment ($\geq 8.0$), and fleet
re-harmonization after any software, model, or configuration change before further
patient-facing use; deterministic version-controlled build.}

\subsection{Data Handling and Record Keeping}
\draftinstr{The seven Physical AI record types (LLM advisory; robot command; sensor
/ telemetry; safety-limit-event; verification-before-generation gate-surface;
human-override; and the federated hash-chained audit trail binding the first six),
retained per 21 CFR \S312.57 with the L0 raw tier preserved (\S10.5).}

\subsection{Protocol Deviations}
\draftinstr{Definition, documentation, assessment, and reporting to the single IRB
and Sponsor-Investigator; immediate-hazard modifications under 21 CFR \S312.30(b),
which for the autonomy-bearing system also trigger the decommissioning and
re-validation controls of \S10.4 and \S10.11.}

\subsection{Publication and Data Sharing Policy}
\draftinstr{Dual deposition (GitHub plus Zenodo, CC BY 4.0) regardless of outcome;
reporting per TRIPOD+AI \cite{Collins2024tripodai} and CONSORT
\cite{Schulz2010consort}; published deterministic seed and repository commit; HIPAA
Safe Harbor de-identification (\S10.5).}

\subsection{Conflict of Interest Policy}
\draftinstr{Disclose and manage financial interests under 21 CFR part 54
\cite{ecfr2026part54}; the Sponsor-Investigator's ChemicalQDevice relationship
managed by the independent oversight of \S10.9 and the capital firewall of \S10.3;
certifications collected before participation and updated through one year after the
study.}
