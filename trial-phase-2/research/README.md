design. The dated 2026 Physical AI FDA-approval and oncology-strategy background

## What grounds the Phase II design

| Evidence stream | Source | Phase II use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Phase 1 predicate results | [`../../trial-protocol/final-protocol/publication`](../../trial-protocol/final-protocol/publication) | the RP2D and device feasibility and safety that establish clinical equipoise for randomization |
| 2026 Physical AI FDA-approval background | [`../../trial-protocol/research`](../../trial-protocol/research) (18 Jun 2026 markdowns) | the regulatory feasibility framing for the multicenter IND/IDE submission |
| 2026 oncology-strategy background | [`../../trial-protocol/research`](../../trial-protocol/research) (19 Jun 2026 markdowns) | the terminally ill / advanced-PDAC strategy and risk-benefit framing |
| Contemporary robotic benchmark | Dutch 2025 nationwide cohort | the external descriptive benchmark; the internal randomized control (Arm B) is the primary comparator |
| Randomized-trial and reporting methods | CONSORT; group-sequential alpha spending; EORTC quality of life; circulating-tumor-DNA | the confirmatory PFS design, the interim analysis, the patient-reported and biomarker endpoints |

## How Phase II differs from the Phase 1 evidence use

Phase 1 used the background to justify a single-arm first-in-human study against an
external benchmark. Phase 2 adds the Phase 1 protocol's own results as the
predicate, and adds the randomized-controlled-trial methodology (powering, group
sequential interim monitoring, fixed-sequence multiplicity control, blinded
independent central review, and circulating-tumor-DNA monitoring) that a
confirmatory efficacy comparison requires. The co-investment evidence (how
operational capital raises accrual speed, retention, simulation fidelity, equity,
and statistical power without touching the endpoints) is developed in the protocol
introduction and oversight sections and aligned with the H. R. 9510 VVUQ financial
standard.
