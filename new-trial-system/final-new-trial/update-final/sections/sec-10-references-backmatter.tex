\bmhead{Build Record}

This paper was produced by one master prompt in a single turn, decomposed by the
model into eight sub-prompts and executed in order, with every generated file
committed and pushed the moment it was written. The eight stages produced, in
order: six mermaid-type figure specifications, four plantuml-type, six d2-type,
four diagrams-type and five graphviz-type, then the draft paper, the full paper,
and this final paper, each with a self-contained Overleaf bundle. Each of the
three paper stages carries sixteen commits: the style file, the main file, the
bibliography, the stage README, eleven section files, a defect-correction pass
over the whole stage, and a repository-update commit. The commit history on the
working branch is the primary record of that sequence, and it is what the
provenance claims of \S\ref{sec:methods} refer to.
