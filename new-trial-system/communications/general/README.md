# general - three brief messages, three perspectives

[![Messages](https://img.shields.io/badge/Messages-3-800020.svg)](https://github.com/kevinkawchak/physical-ai-oncology-trials/tree/main/new-trial-system/communications/general)
[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-v4.6.0-2E2E2E.svg)](https://github.com/kevinkawchak/physical-ai-oncology-trials)

Three short messages for general use, each written from a different perspective
and each intended to be sent with the compiled paper attached. None is addressed
to a named recipient, so any of the three can be reused without editing the
salutation.

| File | Perspective | What it leads with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| general-01-patient-and-caregiver.txt | Patient and caregiver | Why a paper about paperwork matters, in plain language, and the three things that have not changed |
| general-02-clinical-investigator.txt | Clinical investigator and trial operations | Four checks that would falsify the paper's claims, ordered cheapest first |
| general-03-policy-and-press.txt | Policy staff and science press | The 2025 to 2026 Federal record, the capability gap it leaves, and the two disclaimers that must survive any summary |

Each file carries a `NOTES FOR THE AUTHOR` block. The patient message must never
be sent as medical advice and must never imply that anyone can enroll in
anything, because no study is open.
