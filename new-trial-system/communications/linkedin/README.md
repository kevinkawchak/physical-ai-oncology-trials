is written to be posted with the compiled paper attached as a LinkedIn document
rather than linked, because a document is surfaced in the feed and the paper is
the point of the post.

Structure: a two-word hook on the three-day build; the four deliverables as a
list; the prior against new comparison in one paragraph; the measured review
comparison naming Anthropic, OpenAI and Google in their roles; the clinical
arithmetic that makes latency a quantity rather than a preference; the cost
comparison between an article processing charge and thirty-five dollars of
inference; the method as the deliverable; and the seven institutional instruments
the work still needs.

The file ends with a `NOTES FOR THE AUTHOR BEFORE POSTING` block, including the
reply to the objection the post will actually draw, which is that three models
agreeing is not a proof. It is not, the paper says so, and the reply concedes it.
