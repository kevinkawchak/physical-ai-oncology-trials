Dear NCI Cancer Therapy Evaluation Program,

This follows my earlier concept submission inquiry regarding perioperative
daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) in resectable KRAS-mutated pancreatic ductal
adenocarcinoma, submitted as an unaffiliated investigator. The clinical concept
is unchanged. This note documents how the supporting dossier was assembled and
how it can be checked, which is the practical question for a CTEP concept from
an investigator without an institutional regulatory office behind him.

The attached paper sets out the assembly. Twelve modules under 21 CFR 312 in four
days, filed as a combined IND and Investigational Device Exemption because the
device conducts the resection that generates the operative and pathology inputs
to the drug advisory. The paper's Figure 8 is a crosswalk from each codified
21 CFR 312.23 content item to the named section file that satisfies it and to the
repository path where that file is deposited, so a reviewer can pick any content
item and check it against the deposit rather than against a description. Total
regulatory prose is 594,657 characters with 52 distinct 21 CFR cross-references,
22 figures in a single document sequence and 84 section-scoped tables.

The clinical framing is unchanged and stated conservatively. PDAC is the third
leading cause of cancer death in the United States with five-year overall
survival below 13 percent. The contemporary human baseline is the 2025 Dutch
nationwide cohort of 1000 robotic pancreaticoduodenectomies: conversion 10.1
percent, Clavien-Dindo grade III or higher 41.3 percent, ISGPS grade B or C
fistula 24.4 percent, 30-day mortality 3.9 percent. The Phase 1 study is n = 18
on a 3+3 escalation behind a Phase 0 gate of at least 1000 simulations across two
frameworks; the Phase 2 study is n = 220 across eight centers at a target hazard
ratio of 0.60.

Section 7 states the review regime, and states its limits in the same breath.
Model review is not regulatory review. Nothing in this system substitutes for an
institutional review board, an FDA information request, or a data and safety
monitoring board, and the paper says so explicitly.

I remain an unaffiliated independent investigator intending to partner at UC San
Diego Moores Cancer Center at the feasibility stage only; UC San Diego is not a
sponsor, site, or endorser of this work.
