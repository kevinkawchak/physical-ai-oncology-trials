Subsection 10.2 - Five-Domain Patient Control Dashboard (3 to 4
paragraphs plus a comprehensive ASCII dashboard diagram):

Domain 1 - Access: trial-offer rate, days from pathology or genomic
result to trial shortlist, machine-readable eligibility report
coverage, real-time data release rates, travel and work hours saved.
Each metric drawn from
patients/paper/Deep-Research-3/part3\_metrics\_guardrails.md and tied
to HR 4501 + HR 4507. Cite \cite{hhs_right_to_access_2025} and
\cite{rocque2025remote_symptom_monitoring}.

Domain 2 - Decision quality: consent comprehension, decisional-
conflict scores, goal-trial concordance, AI-exclusion appeal success
rate, explanation and provenance coverage. Tied to HR 4503 and the
HTI-1 DSI transparency requirements at \cite{hti1_dsi_fact_sheet_2023}.

Domain 3 - Control-in-action: hospitalization rate, urgent-care or ED
use, alert response time, false-alert burden, serious adverse events
in home or robotics settings. Tied to HR 4504 and HR 4505. Cite
\cite{rocque2025remote_symptom_monitoring},
\cite{gonzalez2025bayesian_urgent_care_nsclc},
\cite{dronca2026cancer_care_beyond_walls}, and
\cite{target2025robotic_bronchoscopy}.

Domain 4 - Fairness and technical quality: declared context of use,
external validation, subgroup performance, calibration monitoring,
update-control disclosure, revalidation after model changes. Tied to
HR 4504 and HR 4506. Cite \cite{fda_ai_regulatory_decision_draft_2025}
and \cite{vorontsov2024virchow}.

Domain 5 (NEW, introduced in this paper) - Physical-AI Execution:
patient-selected robot percentage by category, patient-initiated
procedural modification rate, patient-sponsor direct channel uptake,
American Leadership Index ranking. Tied to HR 4502, HR 4505, and HR
4506. Cite \cite{kawchak_2026_18810541}, \cite{kawchak_2026_18778219},
and \cite{kawchak_2026_19244918}.

Embed a comprehensive ASCII dashboard diagram in a verbatim block
showing the five domains as columns with their metrics as rows. Use
box-drawing characters and keep every line under 80 columns.
