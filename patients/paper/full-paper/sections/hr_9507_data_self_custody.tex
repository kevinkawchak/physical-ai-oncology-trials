% ======================================================================
% SECTION 7: HR 9507 - Cancer Patient Health Data Self-Custody and
% Trial-Selection Act of 2026
% Revision of HHS HIPAA Right-to-Access 2025 and ONC Cures Act Final
% Rule.
% ======================================================================

HR 9507 is a \textit{Revision of the HHS HIPAA Right-to-Access (2025)
framework and the ONC Cures Act Final Rule}, drawing the chain of
prior authority from the HIPAA right-to-access right through the
information-blocking and certification provisions of the Cures Act
\cite{hhs_right_to_access_2025, onc_cures_act_final_rule_page,
federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}. The bill
implements the fourth dimension of the operational definition of
patient control: data and biospecimen governance under HIPAA, broad
consent, and information-blocking penalties.

The current legal stack supplies the foundation. The 21st Century
Cures Act (PL 114-255) established interoperability, information-
blocking, real-world evidence, and the non-device clinical decision
support software carve-out. HHS HIPAA Right-to-Access (2025), the
ASTP/ONC Patient Portals Brief 2024, and the ASTP/ONC Hospital APIs
Brief 2024 establish the access framework. Information-blocking
penalties exist under the federal register disincentives rule but
patient-initiated self-custody of trial-eligibility data is not yet
guaranteed. HR 9507 closes that gap
\cite{rank1_cures_act_2016, onc_cures_act_final_rule_page,
hhs_right_to_access_2025, astp_onc_patient_portals_apps_2024,
astp_onc_hospital_apis_2024,
federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}.

\subsection{Findings and Declarations}

(a) Patient access to health information is a recognized HIPAA
right; the right does not by itself create a patient-controlled
self-custody endpoint suitable for direct grant of trial-sponsor or
AI-matching access \cite{hhs_right_to_access_2025}.

(b) The ASTP/ONC Patient Portals and Hospital APIs briefs document
that adoption of patient-controlled access is uneven across health-
system EHR vendors \cite{astp_onc_patient_portals_apps_2024,
astp_onc_hospital_apis_2024}.

(c) Foundation-model performance in computational pathology
(Virchow, Nature Medicine 2024) supports HR 9507's same-day
pathology release requirement: pathology slides are the dominant
input for trial-eligibility gating in solid tumors, and timely
delivery of high-quality slides materially improves the trial-
matching pipeline \cite{vorontsov2024virchow}.

(d) The prior California SB 892 Clinical Data Protection and
Transparency Act (documented at
\texttt{new-trial/site/03-legislation-data-transparency/physical\_ai\_data\_transparency.tex}
and at
\texttt{national-platform/new\_trial\_psl/03\_sb892\_data\_protection.tex})
provides the State-level baseline that HR 9507 revises into federal
coverage \cite{kawchak_2026_19176370, kawchak_2026_19244918}.

(e) The information-blocking enforcement record is mixed; HR 9507
incorporates the federal register disincentives rule
\cite{federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}.

\subsection{Definitions}

(a) \billterm{Patient health data self-custody} means the patient's
right to hold the patient's own complete oncology medical record,
in machine-readable FHIR format, on a patient-controlled device or
cloud endpoint, with full HIPAA portability across providers,
payers, and trial sponsors.

(b) \billterm{Trial-eligibility data} means the subset of the
patient's medical record that determines eligibility for currently
recruiting oncology trials, including pathology slides and reports,
genomic sequencing reports, prior treatment history, ECOG / PD-L1 /
TMB scores, and current medications. The Virchow pathology
benchmark supports the inclusion of slide-level data, and the
digital-twin module at
\texttt{patient-journey/stage\_03\_digital\_twin.py} provides the
technical baseline \cite{vorontsov2024virchow,
kawchak_2026_19119939}.

(c) \billterm{Same-day release} means pathology and genomic results
released to the patient's self-custody endpoint within the same
calendar day the result is finalized, drawing on Domain 1 of the
four-domain dashboard at
\texttt{patients/paper/Deep-Research-3/part3\_metrics\_guardrails.md}.

\subsection{Patient Health Data Self-Custody Rights}

(a) Right to receive the complete medical record in machine-
readable FHIR format on demand, at zero cost to the patient.

(b) Right to require same-day release of pathology and genomic
results to the patient's self-custody endpoint, drawing on the
ASTP/ONC hospital APIs framework
\cite{astp_onc_hospital_apis_2024}.

(c) Right to grant time-limited, scope-limited access to specific
trial sponsors or AI matching systems (TrialGPT, PRISM,
TrialMatchAI), with revocation taking effect within one hour
\cite{jin2024trialgpt, gupta2024prism, abdallah2026trialmatchai}.

(d) Right to a portable trial-history record that follows the
patient across providers, payers, and States. The data-governance
baseline at
\texttt{national-platform/ich\_e6r3\_adapt/03\_data\_governance.tex}
applies \cite{kawchak_2026_18973368}.

(e) Right to information-blocking penalties under the federal
register disincentives rule where any provider or payer denies the
patient's self-custody request
\cite{federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}.

(f) Right to a permanent, exportable copy of the complete oncology
medical record at end of trial enrollment, with no provider
retention requirement that overrides the patient's self-custody
rights.

\subsection{Implementation}

(a) The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the
Food and Drug Administration, the Office of the National
Coordinator for Health Information Technology, the Office for
Civil Rights, and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services,
shall require all CMS-billable EHR vendors to publish a self-
custody export endpoint within 12 months of enactment.

(b) State-level harmonization shall be complete within 24 months
of enactment.

(c) The preemption rule preserves stronger State-level data-
protection statutes, including the California baseline
\cite{kawchak_2026_19176370}.

\subsection{Reporting and Enforcement}

(a) Annual federal report on per-state, per-payer self-custody
uptake.

(b) Civil penalties under HIPAA enforcement and the information-
blocking disincentives rule
\cite{federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}.

(c) CMS reimbursement penalty for sites that fail to expose the
self-custody endpoint by the 12-month deadline.

(d) The federal Patient Control Dashboard's Domain 1 (Access)
subset anchors the report: same-day pathology release rate, same-
day genomic release rate, end-to-end FHIR export latency, patient-
initiated trial sponsor access grants per quarter, and patient-
initiated revocations within one hour
\cite{hhs_right_to_access_2025, astp_onc_hospital_apis_2024}.

\subsection{Discussion and Limitation}

The bill extends the prior 21st Century Cures Act information-
blocking framework into a positive self-custody right and extends
the HHS HIPAA Right-to-Access 2025 framework with a same-day
release requirement for pathology and genomic results. The Virchow
foundation-model evidence supports the same-day pathology release
because pathology data are dispositive for solid-tumor trial
eligibility \cite{rank1_cures_act_2016, onc_cures_act_final_rule_page,
vorontsov2024virchow, hhs_right_to_access_2025}. The principal
limitation is that the 12-month CMS-billable EHR self-custody
endpoint deadline assumes vendor support at zero cost to the
patient; the Cures Act information-blocking enforcement record is
mixed \cite{federal_register_info_blocking_disincentives_2024}.

\subsection{Patient-Control Framings}

For the individual patient, a 58-year-old female NSCLC patient
matching PAT-2026-0042 receives her PD-L1 65 percent and TMB 14
mutations per megabase scores released to her self-custody endpoint
on the same calendar day. She grants 30-day time-limited access to
TrialGPT and TrialMatchAI for trial discovery (HR 9501), and
revokes that access within one hour after self-booking her
preferred trial \cite{jin2024trialgpt, abdallah2026trialmatchai,
kawchak_2026_19119939}.

For broad adoption, all CMS-billable EHRs must expose a self-
custody export endpoint within 12 months; all United States cancer
patients gain portable trial-eligibility data within 24 months.
The bill positions the United States as Number 1 in the world for
patient health data self-custody, replacing the prior access-only
HIPAA framework with a positive self-custody right
\cite{kawchak_2026_19119939, kawchak_2026_19994945}.
