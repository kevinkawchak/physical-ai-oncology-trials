\vspace{-0.3em}
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\begin{center}

\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth,keepaspectratio]{../images/1.png}
\end{center}

\vspace{-0.3em}
\noindent\dashrule{\textwidth}
\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Surgical Robots}
\end{center}
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{0.3em}
This surgical robot assists your surgeon in performing a minimally invasive robotic prostatectomy through small incisions using precise multi-arm instruments.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Lie face-up on the operating table, place both arms flat on the padded arm rests with palms up, and remain still while anesthesia is administered over approximately 2 minutes.
  \item You will be under general anesthesia for the entire 90--180 minute procedure; the robot's 4 arms operate through 8--12\,mm incisions while the surgeon controls it from a nearby console.
  \item After the robot arms retract, you are moved to recovery where you will awaken in 15--30 minutes; remain lying down until a nurse confirms your vitals.
\end{enumerate}

{\large\bfseries Cobots (Collaborative Robots)}
\end{center}
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{0.3em}
This collaborative robot arm gently positions your arm and assists with automated tissue sample collection for your breast cancer biopsy analysis.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Sit in the adjustable exam chair, rest your arm on the padded support with your palm facing down, and keep your hand relaxed and fingers open for approximately 1 minute.
  \item The cobot moves at a maximum of 250\,mm/s with force-limited contact; hold still for 10--25 minutes while it repositions your arm 2--3 times and collects samples, saying ``Stop'' if you feel discomfort.
  \item The cobot retracts to its home position; remain seated for 2 minutes while results are reviewed, then receive a bandage at the collection site before leaving.
\end{enumerate}

{\large\bfseries Radiotherapy Patient-Positioning Robots}

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{0.3em}
This robotic treatment couch precisely positions your body using six degrees of freedom to align with the radiation beam for your lung tumor treatment.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Lie on the robotic couch in the position practiced during simulation, place your arms in the overhead cradle, and allow the immobilization mask to be fitted over 2--3 minutes.
  \item The couch shifts in 1--3\,mm increments to align you with the beam; breathe normally and remain completely still during the 15--30 minute session, keeping your hands above your head.
  \item When the beam stops, the couch lowers to exit height; wait for the immobilization device to be removed, then sit up slowly using both hands for support.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3em}
This robotic needle-guidance system uses real-time CT imaging to place a biopsy needle with sub-millimeter accuracy into your liver lesion.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Lie on the imaging table face-up with both hands at your sides, hold still while local anesthetic numbs the insertion site for 1--2 minutes, and keep your breathing shallow.

  \item After the needle is withdrawn, press gently on the gauze placed over the site for 3--5 minutes, then remain lying down for 15--30 minutes of monitoring.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]

{\large\bfseries Radiotherapy Motion-Management / Tracking Robots}

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{0.3em}
This motion-tracking system monitors your breathing in real time so the radiation beam activates only when your pancreatic tumor is precisely aligned.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Lie on the treatment couch with arms at your sides, allow a small reflective marker block to be placed on your abdomen, and breathe normally while the system calibrates in 1--2 minutes.
  \item Follow the ceiling-screen breathing guide---inhale when the bar rises, exhale when it falls---for 10--20 minutes; remain still between breaths within the 2--3\,mm tracking tolerance.
  \item After the final beam, the system deactivates; hold still while the marker block is removed from your abdomen, then sit up slowly using both hands on the couch edge.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3em}
This lower-body exoskeleton supports your legs and guides your walking motion to rebuild strength and mobility after your bone cancer surgery.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Stand with your back to the exoskeleton frame, grip both parallel bars firmly, and hold steady while waist, thigh, shank, and foot straps are secured over 2--3 minutes.
  \item Follow the audible step cues and walk between the parallel bars at 0.2--0.5\,m/s for 15--30 minutes; say ``Pause'' to stop at any time, keeping both hands on the bars throughout.
  \item The exoskeleton returns you to a standing rest position; keep gripping the bars while straps are removed in reverse order (feet, shank, thigh, waist), then sit and rest for 5 minutes.
\end{enumerate}
