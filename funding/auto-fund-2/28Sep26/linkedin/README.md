Two replies, identical in substance, each pasted into the LinkedIn conversation
its applicant opened. These are not emails and carry no email addresses: the
applicants wrote on LinkedIn and gave the company no other channel.

## Why LinkedIn and not email

The applicants chose the channel. A reply by email would require finding an
address neither of them gave, and a second, unasked-for contact is the wrong
first impression for an employer to make. Answering in the thread they opened
also keeps their original message one scroll above the reply, which is where a
person looks when they wonder what they asked.

## The two replies

| # | File | Applicant | Characters in body | What it asks for |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | [`linkedin-01-applicant-a-reply.txt`](linkedin-01-applicant-a-reply.txt) | The first to write | Under 1,900 | A resume, the closest role, and consent to be named contingent on award |
| 2 | [`linkedin-02-applicant-b-reply.txt`](linkedin-02-applicant-b-reply.txt) | The second to write | Under 1,900 | The same three things, in the same words |

LinkedIn accepts messages far longer than this. The limit here is the reader's:
a reply that fits on one phone screen and a half is read in full.

## What both replies say, and what neither says

| Both say | Neither says |
|:--|:--|
| There is no open position today | Anything that could be read as an offer or a promise of an interview |
| The company is applying for federal funding for a Phase 1 pancreatic cancer trial | That any funding has been awarded |
| The staffing plan names eleven roles, most of them part-time, funded only on award | A salary; the pay range for any role is given on request, as California law requires |
| The team would work a structured, early-start weekday schedule | Anything about the applicant's background that the company has not been told |
| Where the public role list is | The other applicant's existence |

The last row is deliberate. Each applicant wrote privately, and telling one that
another person also wrote would disclose the second person's approach to a
stranger.

## Privacy

Neither applicant is named in this repository. The files use Applicant A and
Applicant B in the order their messages arrived, and the only bracketed field in
each reply is the first name, which the chief executive types into the LinkedIn
message box and never into a committed file. The public repository is linked in
each reply so that the applicant can read exactly what is and is not published
about the company, and nothing about them is added to it.
