# Form Pack 01: California EDD Employer Registration

**Portal:** EDD e-Services for Business, employer payroll tax account registration
(the online equivalent of the Commercial Employer Account Registration, DE 1).
**Status:** Prepared and held. **Do not submit until a first payroll is
scheduled.** Nothing below has been filed.

---

## When this is filed, and why not today

| Item | Rule | Source |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Trigger | Paying more than $100 in wages to one or more employees in a calendar quarter | [EDD, Am I Required to Register as an Employer?](https://edd.ca.gov/en/Payroll_taxes/Am_I_Required_to_Register_as_an_Employer) |
| Deadline | Within 15 calendar days after the trigger | [EDD, Step 1: Register as an Employer](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/step-1-register-as-an-employer/) |
| Result | An eight-digit state employer payroll tax account number | Same |
| Portal enrollment | A separate e-Services for Business user account, created first | [EDD, Enroll in e-Services for Business](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Enroll_Employer_e-Services_Business/) |

The company has no employees. Registering an employer account with no payroll
creates quarterly return obligations for wages that do not exist, so the account
is registered in the week before a first payroll and not before. The best
practice is to file on the day the first offer is accepted, so the account
number exists before the first pay date rather than fifteen days after it.

## What happens after filing

| Event | Deadline |
|:--|:--|
| Deposit state withholding and state disability insurance | On the schedule the account notice assigns |
| Quarterly contribution return and report of wages | Quarterly, on the EDD calendar |
| New hire report, DE 34 | Within 20 days of each employee's start date; see `form-02-i9-and-new-hire-reporting.md` |

## Before submitting

- Confirm a first offer has been accepted and a first pay date is set.
- Confirm the NAICS codes match the company's SAM.gov entity record, carried
  from `funding/auto-fund/02Sep26/forms/form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md`.
- Confirm workers' compensation coverage is bound before the first employee's
  start date. California requires it of every employer with one employee or
  more.
- Record the account number the portal returns in the company's payroll
  records, not in this repository.
