Two form packs, both completed and both held. A company with two applicants and
no award has no payroll to register and no employee to verify, and filing either
form early would create obligations for wages that do not exist.

## The two packs

| # | File | Portal or form | Trigger | Deadline after trigger |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | [`form-01-edd-employer-registration.md`](form-01-edd-employer-registration.md) | EDD e-Services for Business, employer registration | More than $100 in wages paid in a calendar quarter | 15 calendar days |
| 2 | [`form-02-i9-and-new-hire-reporting.md`](form-02-i9-and-new-hire-reporting.md) | Form I-9, E-Verify if enrolled, DE 34 | An employee's first day of work | 3 business days for I-9 Section 2; 20 days for DE 34 |

## Why both are held

| Pack | Filed today would mean | Filed on time means |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Quarterly returns reporting zero wages, for an account the company does not need yet | An account number that exists before the first pay date |
| 2 | Asking applicants for identity documents before an offer, which California restricts | Documents examined once, after acceptance, in the order the law sets |

The right day to file pack 1 is the day the first offer is accepted. The right
day to begin pack 2 is the employee's first day. Both are written now so that
neither becomes a drafting task on a day that already has a new employee in it.

## What neither pack contains

No applicant's name, address, identification number, or document detail is in
either file, and none ever will be. The packs carry the company's answers and the
field structure; the person's answers are entered in the portal and kept in the
company's private records.
