# Form Pack 02: Form I-9 and the California New Hire Report

**Forms:** USCIS Form I-9, Employment Eligibility Verification; EDD DE 34,
Report of New Employee(s); and the California new hire packet around them.
**Status:** Prepared and held. **Used only after an award is made and an offer is
accepted.** Nothing below has been completed for any person.

---

## The sequence, and why its order is fixed

| # | Step | Deadline | Who completes it |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Written offer, with the pay rate and the pay range for the role | Before acceptance | The company |
| 2 | Wage Theft Prevention Act notice (Labor Code 2810.5) | At hire | The company |
| 3 | Form I-9, Section 1 | No later than the employee's first day of work for pay | The employee |
| 4 | Form I-9, Section 2, documents examined in person or by an authorized alternative | Within 3 business days of the first day of work | The company |
| 5 | E-Verify case, only if the company has enrolled | No later than the third business day after the first day of work | The company |
| 6 | Federal Form W-4 and California Form DE 4 | Before the first payroll | The employee |
| 7 | DE 34, Report of New Employee(s), through e-Services for Business | Within 20 days of the start-of-work date | The company |

Steps 3 to 5 never happen before an offer is accepted. California bars an
employer from using E-Verify to check an applicant who has not been offered
employment (Labor Code 2814), and the company treats Form I-9 the same way: no
document is requested from either applicant during the application stage.

## Form I-9: what the company completes

| Field | Answer |
|:--|:--|
| Employee's first day of employment | The start date in the accepted offer |
| Employer or authorized representative | Kevin Kawchak, Chief Executive Officer |
| Employer's business or organization name | ChemicalQDevice LLC |
| Employer's business address | The company's registered San Diego address |
| Documents | Record the title, issuing authority, number and expiration of what the employee chooses to present; never specify which documents |

The employee chooses the documents. Asking for a particular document, or for
more documents than the form requires, is document abuse under federal law, and
it is avoided by reading the lists aloud and letting the employee pick.

## DE 34: what the company reports

| Field | Answer |
|:--|:--|
| Employer account number | The eight-digit number returned by form pack 01 |
| Federal EIN | The company's EIN |
| Branch code | Blank unless EDD assigned one |
| Business name and address | ChemicalQDevice LLC, the registered San Diego address |
| Contact person and phone | Kevin Kawchak, the company phone |
| Employee's first name, middle initial, last name | From Form I-9 Section 1 |
| Social Security number | From Form W-4 |
| Home address | From Form W-4 |
| Start-of-work date | The first day services were performed for wages |

A late report carries a penalty for each employee not reported. The report is
filed the same week as the first payroll, not at the deadline.

## Retention

| Record | Keep for | Rule |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Form I-9 | 3 years after hire, or 1 year after employment ends, whichever is later | Federal |
| Applications and personnel records | 4 years | Government Code 12946 |
| Payroll tax records | 4 years | EDD employer record retention |

Every one of these is kept in the company's private records. None is ever
committed to this repository.
