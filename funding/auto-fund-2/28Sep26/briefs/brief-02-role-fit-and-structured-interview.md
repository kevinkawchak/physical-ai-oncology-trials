# Role Fit, Pay Ranges, and a Structured Interview

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
Read before any conversation with either applicant. About 1,100 words.

---

## The rule this brief enforces

**Both applicants are asked the same questions, in the same order, and scored
against the same rubric.** A first employer has no policy history to point to.
Its first two conversations become that history, so they are run as if a
reviewer were reading the notes, because one day a reviewer might.

## Pay ranges, for a request under California law

California requires an employer, on an applicant's reasonable request, to give
the pay scale for the position applied for (Labor Code 432.3(c)). The budget
states each role's cost as salary and fringe combined. The ranges below convert
that cost to an annual salary at one full-time equivalent, assuming fringe of 25
to 30 percent of salary. **The fringe assumption is a planning figure; confirm it
with the payroll provider before a range is sent, and send the range for the
role asked about only.**

| Role | Budget per year | FTE | Cost at 1.0 FTE | Salary range at 1.0 FTE |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Director of clinical operations | $54,000 | 0.40 | $135,000 | $103,800 to $108,000 |
| Lead clinical research coordinator | $88,000 | 1.00 | $88,000 | $67,700 to $70,400 |
| Regulatory affairs and quality manager | $52,000 | 0.45 | $115,556 | $88,900 to $92,400 |
| Robotics and physical AI systems engineer | $72,000 | 0.55 | $130,909 | $100,700 to $104,700 |
| LLM verification and model governance lead | $58,000 | 0.40 | $145,000 | $111,500 to $116,000 |
| Data manager and biostatistician | $38,000 | 0.30 | $126,667 | $97,400 to $101,300 |

A part-time role is paid pro rata: the lead coordinator is the only full-time
role on the award. Never ask either applicant what they earn now or earned
before (Labor Code 432.3(a)); the range comes from the budget, not from them.

## The structured conversation, in order

Thirty minutes, by video, scheduled at the start of the business day so the
conversation itself reflects the schedule the role would keep. The same six
questions for both applicants.

| # | Question | What a strong answer shows |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | What did you read of the company's public work, and what made you write? | They engaged with the record, not only the approval headline |
| 2 | Which role on the plan is closest to your experience, and what would you need to learn in the first month? | Accurate self-placement, and a realistic gap |
| 3 | Describe a time you followed a procedure you disagreed with. What did you do, and what did you do afterward? | Compliance first, then a proper route for disagreement |
| 4 | The team works an early-start, scheduled weekday under clinical and regulatory procedures. What would make that hard for you? | Candor; there is no wrong answer except a false one |
| 5 | In a regulated study, who decides when a procedure may be changed? | The procedure's owner and the reviewing body, not the person who finds it inconvenient |
| 6 | The role is funded only if an award is made. How would you want the company to keep you informed? | A realistic expectation of the funding timeline |

Question 3 and question 5 carry the most weight, because they test the first
two of the three priorities the chief executive wrote to the President on
September 12, 2026: obedience before hard work, and adaptation to existing
authority, order, and structure. Question 4 tests the third.

## The rubric

Score each question 0, 1 or 2, write one sentence of evidence beside each score,
and total out of 12. Notes are written during the call, not afterward.

| Score | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| 0 | No answer, or an answer that contradicts the role's delegated tasks |
| 1 | A reasonable answer with no specific example |
| 2 | A specific example that shows the behavior the question asks about |

A total is a record, not a decision. No offer follows any score until an award is
made.

## What may not be asked

| Topic | Rule |
|:--|:--|
| Current or past pay | Barred by Labor Code 432.3 |
| Criminal history before a conditional offer | Barred for employers of five or more by Government Code 12952; followed here anyway |
| Age, family plans, health, national origin, religion | Protected characteristics under the Fair Employment and Housing Act |
| Citizenship or immigration status | Work authorization is verified on Form I-9 only after an offer is accepted |
| The other applicant | Never mentioned to either |

## Worker classification

Both roles, if offered, are employee positions paid through payroll. California
applies the ABC test to decide whether a worker is an employee or an independent
contractor (Labor Code 2775), and a research coordinator or engineer who works
the company's schedule, under its procedures, in its usual course of business
fails every prong of the contractor side. Neither applicant is offered a
contractor arrangement as a way to start sooner.

## Records

Resumes, notes and scores go in the company's private applicant log, never in
this repository, and are kept for four years (Government Code 12946).
