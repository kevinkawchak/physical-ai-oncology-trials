## The one assumption, stated once

The staffing plan prices each role as salary and fringe combined, and publishes
no fringe rate. A pay range for a job applicant must be a salary range, so brief
2 converts cost to salary with an assumed fringe of 25 to 30 percent. That is
the only number in these three briefs that is not carried directly from a
source, and brief 2 says so in bold beside the table.
