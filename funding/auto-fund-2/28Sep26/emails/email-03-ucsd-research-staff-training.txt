Dear Moores Clinical Trials Office, ACTRI Clinical Trial Support Services, and Office of IRB Administration,

I wrote to Moores Cancer Center earlier asking for a routing decision on a proposed investigator-initiated Phase 1 study of perioperative daraxonrasib with a staged robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. This letter asks something much smaller, and it does not depend on that routing decision.

Two people have asked, on their own initiative, to work for my company. If the company's federal applications are funded, either could fill a research operations role on the study's staffing plan, such as clinical research coordinator or regulatory and quality manager. They would be employees of the company, not of the university.

My question is what training such a person would need before being engaged in research reviewed by the university's IRB, and whether any of it is open to them before then.

1) What human subjects protection and Good Clinical Practice training does the university accept from research personnel who are not university employees, and is completion through the CITI Program acceptable?
2) Does ACTRI offer coordinator orientation or training that non-university staff may attend, and if so, on what terms?
3) If a non-university person were engaged in research at a university site, which office should I ask about the reliance or affiliation arrangement that would be required?

I am not asking the university to agree to host the study, to review a protocol, or to endorse anything. The answer decides what I ask the two people to complete on their own schedule, at the company's expense, so that nothing about their training becomes a delay later.
