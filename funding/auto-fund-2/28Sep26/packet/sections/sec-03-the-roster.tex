\section{Eleven Roles, and the Six an Applicant Can Fill}

The staffing plan behind the company's federal applications is published as
document 14 of the La Jolla site package \cite{movein2026}. It names eleven
roles at 3.95 award-funded full-time equivalents, prices them at \$521,000 a
year in salary and fringe combined, and fits them inside a \$700,000 annual
direct cost. Nine of the eleven are part-time, and the fractions are what make
an eleven-person site affordable on that budget.

Table 3 reads the plan from the point of view of an unsolicited applicant. Four
roles require a California clinical license and would come from the host
institution. One is held by the chief executive. The remaining six require
training and experience but no license, and those are the six the two
inquiries could fill.

\subsection{What the six roles do in the robotic Phase 1}

The trial the roles would run is an open-label, single-arm, 3+3 dose escalation
of perioperative daraxonrasib at 160, 220 and 300 mg with a 28-day
dose-limiting toxicity window, in up to 18 treated participants undergoing a
staged eight-arm robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy \cite{onpremwhippl,phase1ind}.
Each open role owns a part of that design that no other role can cover.

The lead clinical research coordinator runs consent, screening and every study
visit, and is the only full-time role on the award. The director of clinical
operations closes the activation checklist and hosts monitoring visits. The
regulatory affairs and quality manager holds the correspondence with the review
board and the agency. The systems engineer and site safety officer owns the 3 N
per-arm and 18 N cumulative force limits, the 3 ms cross-arm stop and the 500 ms
system stop. The model governance lead holds the boundary that keeps the
on-premises language model advisory only. The data manager and biostatistician
convenes each cohort review and locks the database.

\subsection{Why the licensed roles are never recruited this way}

A site principal investigator, a medical oncology sub-investigator, an
investigational drug pharmacist and a research nurse are licensed clinicians
whose time on the trial is contributed under the site agreement. Recruiting any
of them through an unsolicited message would bypass the institution that
credentials them, and the sponsor-investigator firewall in the site package
requires that every investigator decision sit with the site rather than with the
company \cite{movein2026}.
