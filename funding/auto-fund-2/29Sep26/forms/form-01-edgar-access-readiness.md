# Form Pack 01: EDGAR Access Readiness

**Portal:** SEC EDGAR Filer Management, Form ID application for EDGAR access.
**Status:** Prepared and held. **Submit only when a first filing is scheduled.**
Nothing below has been submitted.

---

## Why this is prepared now and not filed

Correspondence with an SEC regional office needs no EDGAR access. A filing does.
The company prepared, and deliberately left unfiled, a Form D for a Regulation D
Rule 506(b) offering in the first block of funding actions
(`funding/auto-fund/03Sep26/forms/form-01-reg-d-506b-form-d.md`). A Form D is due
within fifteen days after a first sale, and EDGAR access has to exist before
then. An office that is taking a request from this company seriously should not
later find the company unable to file on time.

| Item | Fact | Source |

| Administrators | At least two, or at least one for an individual or single-member company, and never more than twenty | [SEC, Understand EDGAR Next Roles](https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/filer-support-resources/how-do-i-guides/understand-edgar-next-roles) |
