on time, and it has nothing to do with the external-standard request.

| # | File | Portal | Trigger | Deadline after trigger |

## Why it is held

The company has no public securities and no offering underway. The Form D it
prepared in the first block is unfiled because no sale has occurred. Applying for
EDGAR access with no filing in view would put a question on the record the
company has no reason to create, so the pack is complete and waiting.

## Why it belongs to this day

A company in correspondence with an SEC office should be able to file with the
Commission on time if the need arises. Readiness costs an hour; a missed Form D
deadline costs credibility with the one federal agency the company is asking to
take it seriously.

## What the pack does not contain

No credential, recovery code, EIN, or CIK is written into this repository. The
pack names the fields and the company's answers; the identifiers are entered in
the portal and kept in the company's credential vault.
