\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

The chief executive of ChemicalQDevice asked the SEC San Francisco Regional
Office that the company's oncology clinical trial research be listed as an
external standard for substantial oncology clinical trial research, on the basis
that the company's LLM papers have been used extensively by OpenAI and
Anthropic. The office has written that it is taking the request ``very
seriously'' \cite{secsf2026}. This packet answers that update.

It answers with a record rather than an argument. Since June 2025 the company
has deposited eleven works on one program with persistent identifiers, built
around daraxonrasib independently of its developer and simultaneously with the
developer's Phase 1/2 and Phase 3 trials, before and after the FDA approved the
molecule on August 26, 2026 as Rasonque \cite{fdarasonque2026,ctgov001,ctgov302}.
The packet lists every identifier with its date, sets the developer's trial
calendar beside the company's, and scores the record against eight criteria any
external standard would have to meet. Three are met, three are met in part, and
two are not yet met; each of the five gaps has a named next action.

Five letters go out today. One answers the SEC office, one asks the FDA's
Oncology AI Program which federal venue owns the clinical question
\cite{fdaoceai2026}, and three tell OpenAI, Anthropic and Revolution Medicines
that the request names or concerns them, before they hear it anywhere else.

\subsection*{How to read this}

A reader with five minutes takes \S1, the update and the record, and \S2, the
action register with the state of the request drawn as a machine with guards.

A reader weighing the request takes \S3, which sets the developer's trials and
the company's papers on one calendar, and \S4, which scores the record against
the eight criteria and shows which part of the record carries each one.

A funder takes \S5, the six checkable quantities and the robotic Phase 1 they
support. \S6 carries the positioning constraints, the method, and the
references, every one of which is a clickable link.
