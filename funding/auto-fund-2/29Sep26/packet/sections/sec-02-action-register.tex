\subsection{The request as a state machine}

A request to a regulator moves through states the company does not control, and
it is easy to describe a state as further along than it is. Figure 4 draws the
states, marks the one the request is in today, and writes on each transition the
condition that moves it. Only two of those conditions are in the company's hands:
supplying the record, and correcting it if a named company asks.

\subsection{Who each letter reaches, and why}

The reply to the office copies the Los Angeles Regional Office, whose region
includes San Diego, and the Office of the Advocate for Small Business Capital
Formation, because the request comes from a small business with a capital
formation question behind it \cite{secregional2026,secoasb2026}. The routing
letter to the FDA's Oncology AI Program copies the Oncology Center of Excellence
and the Office of Combination Products, because the program behind the question
would, if it ever reached a submission, be a combination of a drug, a robotic
device and advisory software \cite{fdaoceai2026,fdaoce2026,fdacombination2026}.

Row 7 is readiness, not a filing. The company prepared, and left unfiled, a Form
D for a private offering in the first block of funding actions; a Form D is due
within fifteen days after a first sale, and EDGAR access must exist before then
\cite{secformd2026,secformid2026}. An office that is taking a request from this
company seriously should never find the company unable to file on time.

\subsection{What the three notices are for}

Letters 3, 4 and 5 ask for nothing. They exist because a company that names
third parties in a request to a federal office tells them first. OpenAI and
Anthropic are each told what the company's own repository records about their
tools, and each is invited to correct the request's description of them, with
the correction copied to the office the same day
\cite{tripartisan2026,anthropicnews2026}.
Revolution Medicines is told that independent research on its molecule is
referenced, that no relationship of any kind exists, and that the request
concerns nothing about the company or its securities \cite{revmedapproval2026}.
The SEC is deliberately not copied on that letter.
