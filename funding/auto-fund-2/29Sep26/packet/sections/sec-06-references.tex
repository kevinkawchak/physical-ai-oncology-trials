\subsection{Positioning constraints}

The SEC San Francisco Regional Office has written that it is taking the
company's request ``very seriously.'' That is a statement about a request. It is
not a determination, a listing, an endorsement, or a decision, and nothing in
this packet describes it as one \cite{secsf2026}. The Commission does not set
clinical research standards, and the company has said so to the office in
writing.

and Anthropic is the company's own, made as the basis of its request. The
company can document its own use of the two developers' tools; it cannot
document either developer's internal use of its papers. No agreement,
sponsorship, investment, or endorsement of any kind exists with either company,
and each has been invited to correct the statement \cite{tripartisan2026}.

No agreement, supply arrangement, or right of reference of any kind exists with
Revolution Medicines, and the company holds no nonpublic information about it.
Rasonque is approved for metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma after prior
systemic therapy \cite{fdarasonque2026}; the perioperative use this program
proposes remains investigational. No investigational new drug application has
been submitted \cite{ecfr2026part312}, and no patient has been treated. The
company has no public securities and no offering underway, and nothing in the
day's capital instruction is investment advice.
