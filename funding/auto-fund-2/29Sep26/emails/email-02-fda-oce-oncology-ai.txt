Dear Oncology AI Program,

I am writing to ask for a routing answer rather than a review, and I have kept this letter short for that reason. I have copied the Oncology Center of Excellence and the Office of Combination Products because the program behind the question would, if it ever reached a submission, be a combination product.

ChemicalQDevice is a one-person San Diego company. Since June 2025 it has deposited a dated, public record of AI-developed oncology trial documents built around daraxonrasib: an identification study, a survival simulation, a Phase 1 protocol for a staged robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy with perioperative daraxonrasib, a Phase 2 protocol, an investigational new drug application, and several funding applications. The work was developed independently of Revolution Medicines, and simultaneously with its Phase 1/2 and Phase 3 trials. On August 26, 2026 the FDA approved daraxonrasib as Rasonque for metastatic pancreatic cancer. The perioperative use my documents propose is not the approved setting and remains investigational.

The question arises because I asked the SEC San Francisco Regional Office whether the company's oncology trial research could be listed as an external standard for substantial oncology clinical trial research, on the basis that the company's LLM papers have been used extensively by OpenAI and Anthropic. The office has written that it is taking the request very seriously. I have told the office I understand that the Commission does not set clinical research standards, and that I am writing to you in parallel.

My questions are about venue only.
1) Does the Oncology AI Program accept inquiries from a small company about how an independent body of AI-developed oncology trial documents could be evaluated, referenced, or used as an external benchmark, and if not, which office does?
2) Is there an existing FDA mechanism, such as a pilot, a public workshop, or a recognized consensus standards process, where a question of this kind belongs?
3) For the Office of Combination Products: if the program's device and software components are ever submitted, is a Pre-Request for Designation the right first step, or should it wait until a clinical site sponsors the concept?

I am not asking the FDA to review, endorse, or comment on any document, and I will not describe any reply as an FDA position. If the answer is that no venue exists today, that is a useful answer and I will say so to the SEC office.

Thank you for considering it.

Sincerely,
