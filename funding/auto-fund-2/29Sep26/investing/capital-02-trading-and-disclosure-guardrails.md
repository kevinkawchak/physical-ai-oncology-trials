# Capital Instruction 02: Trading and Disclosure Guardrails

**ChemicalQDevice LLC corporate brokerage account, and the chief executive
personally.** Direction of the sole member and chief executive. Not investment
advice, not a recommendation to any other person, and not an offer or
solicitation. Nothing below is an order.

---

## Why a trading rule belongs on this day

This day's letters go to an SEC regional office and to the investor relations
office of a public company. The company holds no material nonpublic information
about Revolution Medicines, and letter 5 says so. The guardrails below make sure
that statement stays true, and that no trade by the company or its chief
executive could ever be read against the correspondence. They cost nothing and
they are adopted before letter 5 leaves.

## The restricted list

| # | Security | Why it is restricted | Restriction |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Revolution Medicines, Inc. common stock (Nasdaq: RVMD) | The company writes to its investor relations office and publishes research on its approved drug | No purchase, sale, short sale, or option of any kind |
| 2 | Options, warrants, or derivatives on RVMD | Same | No position of any kind |
| 3 | Any security of a public company the company writes to about a nonpublic matter | A letter can create the appearance of access | Added to this list the day the letter is sent |
| 4 | Any secondary-market interest in OpenAI or Anthropic, through a fund, tender, or special purpose vehicle | Both are named in the company's request to the SEC office | No purchase while the request is open |

Neither OpenAI nor Anthropic has public stock. Row 4 exists because interests in
private companies are traded through intermediaries, and a company that names two
private companies in a request to a regulator should not buy into either while
the request is open.

## The rules

| Rule | Applies to | Duration |
|:--|:--|:--|
| No trading in any restricted security | The company and the chief executive personally | While the security is on the list, and for 30 days after it comes off |
| The company's reserve holds U.S. Treasury obligations, one short Treasury fund, and a government money market sweep, and nothing else | The company | Standing, from `funding/auto-fund/02Sep26/investing/capital-01-treasury-ladder.md` |
| No public statement about RVMD's prospects, price, or trials | The company and the chief executive | Standing |
| No use of any information from any recipient's reply in any trade | The company and the chief executive | Standing |
| Pre-clearance: any personal trade in a health care or artificial intelligence security is written down, with its reason, the day before it is placed | The chief executive | While any of this day's requests is open |

## What this changes in the account today

Nothing is bought or sold. The company's reserve already holds only Treasury
obligations and government funds, so no position conflicts with the list. The
instruction is a written rule that the broker's compliance note and the
company's own file both carry.

## The one entry to make

| Item | Entry |
|:--|:--|
| Company file | This instruction, dated the day it is approved, with the restricted list |
| Brokerage | A note in the account's message center: "The account holder maintains a restricted list; no trade in RVMD or its derivatives is to be entered." |
| Chief executive's personal accounts | The same note, if the broker permits one |

## The three checks before this is approved

1. Confirm the company's reserve and the chief executive's personal accounts hold no RVMD shares or derivatives today. If either does, stop and take advice before any letter to Revolution Medicines is sent.
2. Confirm no one at the company has received any nonpublic information from Revolution Medicines, OpenAI, or Anthropic.
3. Confirm this instruction is adopted before letter 5 is sent.
