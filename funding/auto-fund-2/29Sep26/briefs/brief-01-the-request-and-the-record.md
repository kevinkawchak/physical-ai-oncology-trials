oncology clinical trial research be listed as an external standard for

what is quoted from the office anywhere in this repository.

## What independence and simultaneity mean here

**Independent** means the company had no relationship with the developer, no
access to its data, and no right of reference to its filings. Every input to
the company's work was public.

**Simultaneous** means the company's work was done while the developer's trials
were running, not after their results were known. Entries 1 and 2 predate the
Phase 3 readout by eleven and nine months. Entries 3 to 7 were deposited after
the readout and before the approval. Entries 8 to 10 bracket the approval.

## What the record shows, and what it does not

| It shows | It does not show |
|:--|:--|
| A dated, public, continuous body of work on one molecule | That any document is correct in every particular |
| A survival simulation nine months before a Phase 3 readout that landed nearby | That the simulation validated the trial or the trial the simulation |
| That the work was done independently of the developer | Any endorsement by the developer, the FDA, or any reviewer |
| A method that other groups could reproduce from the repository | That any group has done so |

The simulation and the trial differ in three material ways: 1000 simulated
patients against 241 enrolled in the relevant comparison, a combination against
a single agent, and a KRAS G12C model against a primarily G12D and G12V trial
population. The proximity of the two results is a chronology observation, and it
is described that way everywhere.

## What would strengthen the record

An independent group reproducing any one deposited result from the repository,
a human peer review of the Phase 1 protocol by a clinical investigator, and the
FDA venue answer that letter 2 asks for. None of the three exists today, and the
company does not describe any of them as existing.
