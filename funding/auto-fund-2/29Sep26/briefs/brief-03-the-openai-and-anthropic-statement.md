# The OpenAI and Anthropic Statement

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
For anyone who asks what "used extensively" means. About 650 words.

---

## The sentence

The basis of the request to the SEC office is that the company's LLM papers
have been used extensively by OpenAI and Anthropic. The sentence is the company's
own. It appears word for word in every letter that mentions it and is never
strengthened, shortened, or attributed to anyone else.

## What the company can document

The repository publishes the division of work between the three frontier models
the company uses, and every deposited work carries a statement of the tools used
to produce it. The table below is taken from that record and from nothing else.

| Developer | Tool | What the repository records it doing |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Anthropic | Claude Code | Investigational new drug applications; Phase 1 and Phase 2 pancreatic cancer trial protocols; FDA and ICH regulation adaptations; legislation revisions; trial site and sponsor simulations; machine-readable diagrams; large-scale repository platforms |
| OpenAI | Deep research | The June 2025 work of forty pancreatic cancer meta-analyses, over 400,000 words, in which daraxonrasib was first identified |
| OpenAI | ChatGPT Codex | Verification, validation and uncertainty quantification of Claude Code outputs; meta-analyses of clinical trial papers; first peer review |
| Google | Gemini | Second peer review; the June 2025 ranking of a daraxonrasib combination first of forty candidates |

Commits that Claude Code produced in the repository carry a co-author trailer
naming it, and the deposited papers state the model they were adapted with. A
reader can therefore check the Anthropic rows against the commit history.

## What the company cannot document

The company cannot see inside either developer. It cannot document whether, or
how, OpenAI or Anthropic have used its papers internally, and it does not claim
knowledge of either company's training data, evaluations, or research. Where the
request says the papers have been used extensively by the two companies, the
evidence the company can put forward is the record above, and letters 3 and 4
say exactly that to each company.

## The correction pathway

| If a developer replies | The company does |
|:--|:--|
| That the description is accurate | Records the reply as received and tells the SEC office in the same thread |
| That the description is inaccurate in part | Sends a written correction to the SEC office the same day, copying the developer |
| That it prefers not to be named | Withdraws the name from the request in writing, copying the developer |
| Nothing | Changes nothing, and does not describe silence as agreement |

## What the statement never becomes

| Never | Because |
|:--|:--|
| "Endorsed by OpenAI and Anthropic" | Neither company has endorsed anything |
| "In partnership with" either company | No agreement of any kind exists |
| A logo, a trademark, or a product name used as a badge | Neither company has licensed its marks to this company |
| A claim about the other developer in a letter to one of them | Each letter speaks only about its recipient |

## Why the company made the statement at all

The request is that the company's oncology trial research serve as an external
standard. A standard is only as useful as the systems that can read it, and the
company's work is written to be read and extended by frontier language models.
The two companies named are the developers whose tools produced and reviewed
most of it. That is the claim the record supports, and it is the claim the
company stands behind.
