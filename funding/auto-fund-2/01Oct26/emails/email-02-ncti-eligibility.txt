I am writing with one eligibility question about the Commercialization Readiness Pilot that the National Commercialization and Translation Institute runs for NSF. It holds no NSF SBIR or STTR award today. I have read that the pilot's competition is open to active or recent NSF SBIR/STTR Phase II and IIB awardees, and that it will select six to eight companies for milestone-based awards of $1 million to $3.75 million.

My question is only this: is there any route into the pilot, now or in a later cohort, for a company that does not yet hold an NSF Phase II or IIB award, or is a Phase II the firm prerequisite?
