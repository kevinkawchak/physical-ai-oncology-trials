Dear NIH SBIR and STTR Program Staff,

I wrote to this office recently with a question about naming prospective personnel in a Phase I application. I am writing again to disclose something before it could become a question, and I have copied the NCI SBIR Development Center and NSF's SBIR program so that both agencies hold the same text.

ChemicalQDevice has submitted, or is about to submit, a Project Pitch to NSF under NSF 26-510. I understand that SBIR rules do not permit essentially equivalent work to be funded by two agencies, so I want to set out how the two scopes differ.
1) The NSF Pitch concerns the verification methods for a governed, advisory-only language model: how its outputs are checked by independent models, how its boundaries are tested, and how its audit trail is kept. It involves no clinical trial and no patient data.
2) The planned NIH application concerns the clinical use of that layer inside a Phase 1 trial of perioperative daraxonrasib with a staged robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy, in which the layer is one component among several and the trial belongs to a site investigator.

If both were ever funded, the NIH work would build on the NSF results rather than repeat them, and each application would disclose the other as pending or awarded, as the rules require.

My question is whether the office would advise any further step now, before either application is submitted, to keep the two scopes clear.

Thank you.
