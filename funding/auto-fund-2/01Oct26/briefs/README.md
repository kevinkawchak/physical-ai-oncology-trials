Three briefs for the three questions an NSF reader will ask: can the company
enter the pilot, what exactly is NSF being asked to fund, and how does the work
score against NSF's review criteria.

## The three briefs

| # | File | Question it answers | Reader | Length |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | [`brief-01-the-eligibility-gate.md`](brief-01-the-eligibility-gate.md) | Where does the company stand against each NSF tier and pilot, and what is the route? | NSF program staff, the NCTI team | About 650 words |
| 2 | [`brief-02-what-nsf-funds.md`](brief-02-what-nsf-funds.md) | How is one program split between NSF, NIH and private capital without overlap? | Any reviewer | About 600 words |
| 3 | [`brief-03-merit-review-map.md`](brief-03-merit-review-map.md) | How does the work map to Intellectual Merit, Broader Impacts and commercial impact? | An NSF reviewer | About 600 words |

## The one sentence all three agree on

The company can take the first step on the route to NSF's multi-million-dollar
pilots today, and cannot take any later step until an award it does not yet
hold. Every brief says so, in its own terms.
