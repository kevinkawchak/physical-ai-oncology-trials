# Form Pack 02: Research.gov Organization Registration

**Portal:** Research.gov, NSF account registration and Register a New
Organization. **Status:** Prepared. **Complete before the Project Pitch** if the
company has no Research.gov organization record; if it has one, confirm it and
submit the Pitch.

---

## The prerequisite, in order

| # | Step | Where | Why |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | An active SAM.gov registration with a Unique Entity Identifier | SAM.gov | NSF registers only organizations with a valid UEI and an active SAM registration |
| 2 | An individual NSF account for the chief executive, with an NSF ID | [apps.research.gov/account/registration](https://apps.research.gov/account/registration) | The organization is registered by a signed-in individual |
| 3 | The organization record, through Register a New Organization | Research.gov, signed in | The Pitch and any proposal are submitted by the organization |
| 4 | Roles assigned: Principal Investigator, and Authorized Organizational Representative | Research.gov, organization functions | A proposal is submitted by the AOR and led by the PI |

SAM.gov registration can take several weeks. The company's entity record was
validated in the first block of funding actions
(`funding/auto-fund/02Sep26/forms/form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md`); confirm
it is still active on the day of this step.

`rgov@nsf.gov` or 1-800-381-1532. Letter 1 of this day already copies that
address.
