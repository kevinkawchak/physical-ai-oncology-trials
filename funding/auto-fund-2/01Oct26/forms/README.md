Every field leaves room, deliberately. A Pitch written to the last character is a
Pitch that cannot be corrected on the day of submission without cutting
something that mattered.

## What the Pitch proposes

A verification method for a governed, advisory-only language model in robotic
oncologic surgery: a boundary test suite, a cross-model verification yield
study on synthetic cases, a credibility battery extended from simulation to
advisory text, and a hardware-in-the-loop timing test. It proposes no clinical
trial and uses no patient data, because NSF's SBIR program funds neither.

## Why a Pitch at all, given the pilot's gate

The multi-million-dollar pilots NSF has announced are open to Phase II
awardees. A Phase II follows a Phase I, and a Phase I follows an invited Pitch.
The Pitch is therefore the first step on the only published route to the pilot
the inquiry mentioned, and it is the one step the company can take today.
