## Why it belongs to this day

The pilot the NSF inquiry mentioned sits beyond a Phase II, and the tiers beside
it carry matching requirements. A match is proven with records kept from the
first dollar. Opening the ledger on the first day the route begins costs nothing
and prevents a reconstruction later.
