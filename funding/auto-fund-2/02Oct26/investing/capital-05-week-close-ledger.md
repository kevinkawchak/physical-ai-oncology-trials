# Capital Instruction 05: The Week-Close Ledger

**ChemicalQDevice LLC.** Direction of the sole member and chief executive. Not
investment advice, not a recommendation to any other person, and not an offer or
solicitation. This instruction places no order.

---

## Why a week-close entry

Four capital instructions were approved this week, one a day, and each set a
rule the account must keep. A rule that is set and never checked becomes a rule
nobody can show was kept. This instruction checks all four at the close of the
week's last session, records the result, and buys and sells nothing.

## The four instructions, checked

| # | Instruction | Rule it set | The check at week's close | Pass if |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | Contingent payroll earmark | Rung A of the Treasury ladder designated as the payroll earmark, with a $40,000 ceiling | Rung A's par, and whether a top-up bid was entered | Par is at least $40,000, or a top-up bid is recorded for the next 13-week auction |
| 02 | Trading and disclosure guardrails | The restricted list: RVMD and its derivatives; any secondary interest in OpenAI or Anthropic | Every trade confirmation of the week against the list | No trade touched the list, and the list is current |
| 03 | Quarter-end policy review | Five checks on the last session of the quarter | The review record dated September 30 | All five checks are marked, and any roll order is recorded |
| 04 | Match ledger | A ledger for third-party matching funds, opened at $0 | The ledger's balance and its firewall column | Balance is $0, or every entry carries a firewall note |

## One addition to the restricted list

Instruction 02 adds any public company the company writes to about a nonpublic
matter on the day the letter is sent. This week's letters went to federal
and state offices, two universities, two private AI developers, and one public
company already on the list. No new public company was written to, so no row is added. The
check is recorded anyway, because a list that is not checked cannot be shown to
be current.

## What is not in the ledger

| Item | Why not |
|:--|:--|
| Any federal award money | There is none, and the funds-separation rule keeps any future award in its own account |
| The $5,900,000 private frame | A plan, not a completed raise; nothing has been received |
| Either applicant | No offer has been made, so no payroll exists to fund |

## Today's entry

| Field | Entry |
|:--|:--|
| Date | The day this instruction is approved |
| Instructions checked | 01, 02, 03, 04 |
| Result | Each marked pass or fail, with one sentence of evidence |
| Orders entered this week | Any top-up bid under 01 and any roll under 03, each with its auction and settlement date; otherwise none |
| Next check | The last session of next week, under the weekly cadence |

## The one rule this instruction adds

A capital instruction is checked on the Friday of the week it is approved and on
every Friday after, in the cadence's record block from 3:30 to 4:00 p.m. Pacific.
A failed check is corrected by the instruction that set the rule, never by a new
trade outside it.
