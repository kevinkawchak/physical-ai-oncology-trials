## Why letter 1 goes to NCI rather than back to the program office

NIH received two letters this week: a personnel question on Monday and an
overlap disclosure on Thursday. Two follow-ups to one office in one week would be
two too many. One consolidated letter answers for both, and it goes to the NCI
SBIR Development Center directly, because the application would be assigned to
the National Cancer Institute, with the program office and SEED copied as
before.

## How to send one

1. Open the `.txt` file on GitHub, select everything between `=== BODY ===` and `=== END BODY ===`, and copy it.
2. Paste into a reply inside the original thread in iOS Mail. Each paragraph is one line in the file, so the phone wraps it to the screen with no stray breaks.
3. Confirm the `TO`, `CC` and `SUBJECT` lines against the file.
4. Attach the files named under `ATTACHMENTS COMPILED FROM THIS DIRECTORY`.
5. Work through `BEFORE SENDING` line by line.
