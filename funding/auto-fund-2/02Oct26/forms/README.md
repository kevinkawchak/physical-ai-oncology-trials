## Why a check belongs on Friday

The cadence's Friday is the day of record. A registry record is part of the
company's record, and every application this week pointed toward will cite it.
Checking it on the day the week is closed means the next week starts with a
record known to be current rather than one assumed to be.

## The two triggers

| Trigger | Field it changes |
|:--|:--|
| An offer accepted by anyone, including either applicant | Number of employees |
| Any equity sold to a venture, hedge fund or private equity investor | Ownership |

Neither can occur this week, and neither is expected before an award.
