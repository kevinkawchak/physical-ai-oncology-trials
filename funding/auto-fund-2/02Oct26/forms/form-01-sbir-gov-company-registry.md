# Form Pack 01: SBIR.gov Company Registry, the Week-End Check

**Portal:** SBIR.gov, Company Registry, operated by the U.S. Small Business
Administration. **Status:** Prepared. **Complete on day 5**, in the 8:00 a.m.
block, before the next application is drafted.

---

## Why this form, this week

Every SBIR application names the company's registry record. NIH asks for the
registration certificate, a PDF carrying the Small Business Concern (SBC)
Control ID, to be attached to each application, and the NSF solicitation asks
for the same identifier at the full proposal stage. This week the company
pitched to NSF, disclosed that pitch to NIH, and asked NCI for a program
director. Before any of those becomes an application, the record it will cite
must be current, and a week that brought two applicants and a private-capital
question is a week in which two of its answers could change.

## The fields to confirm

| Field | This week's answer | Changed this week? |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Legal name and UEI | ChemicalQDevice LLC, exactly as in SAM.gov | No |
| Address | The registered San Diego address, exactly as in SAM.gov | No |
| Ownership | 100 percent owned by one U.S. citizen, the sole member | No |
| Number of employees, including affiliates | 1 | No. The two applicants are not employees, and no offer has been made |
| Ownership by venture capital, hedge funds or private equity | None | No. The $5,900,000 private frame is a plan, not a completed raise |
| Affiliates | None | No |
| Company website and contact | The company's public site; `kevink@chemicalqdevice.com` | No |

If every row still reads "No", the record is current and nothing is submitted.
Download the certificate again only if the portal shows a later revision date
than the copy on file.

## The two rows that could change, and what triggers each

| Row | Trigger | What to do before the next application |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Number of employees | An offer is accepted by anyone, including either applicant | Update the count on the day the person starts, and re-download the certificate |
| Venture, hedge fund or private equity ownership | Any equity is sold to such an investor | Update before the next application, and confirm each agency's ownership rule against its current solicitation, because the agencies do not all apply the same rule |

Neither trigger can occur this week. Both are listed so that the record is
checked on the day either one does.

## Where the certificate is used

| Application | Where the SBC Control ID goes |
|:--|:--|
| NIH SBIR Phase I | The SBIR/STTR Information form of the application, with the certificate PDF attached |
| NSF SBIR Phase I | The company information of the full proposal, after an invitation |

## Before completing

- Sign in with the same Login.gov identity used for SAM.gov if the portal asks
  for one.
- Confirm the legal name and address against SAM.gov character for character.
- Keep the certificate PDF with the data room copy of the SAM.gov record, never
  in this repository.
