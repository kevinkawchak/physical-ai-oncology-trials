Two replies, identical in text, each pasted into the LinkedIn conversation its
applicant opened, below the company's reply of September 28. Each is sent only
if that applicant has answered.

## The rule for an applicant

An applicant is not a federal office, and the follow-up rule for offices does
not apply. The company wrote first on Monday; the next message belongs to the
applicant. If an applicant has answered, the company replies with the next step.
If an applicant has not answered, the company sends nothing and the thread stays
open. Chasing a person who wrote once and then went quiet would turn an
unsolicited inquiry into an unsolicited pursuit.

## What both replies say, and what neither says

| Both say | Neither says |
|:--|:--|
| The next step is a thirty-minute video conversation with the same questions for everyone | Anything that could be read as an offer, a start date, or a salary commitment |
| Three 8:00 a.m. Pacific slots, and why the time is early | That another person has also written |
| There is still no open position; every role is funded only on award | That any award has been made |
| Nothing that names the applicant is submitted without written agreement | Anything the applicant has not already told the company |
| The pay range for the role named is available on request | A pay figure, unless the applicant asked for one |

## How the conversation is run

From the six questions and the 0 to 2 rubric in
[`../../28Sep26/briefs/brief-02-role-fit-and-structured-interview.md`](../../28Sep26/briefs/brief-02-role-fit-and-structured-interview.md),
in order, with notes written during the call. Questions 3 and 5 test the first
two priorities of the September 12 message, and question 4 tests the third. A
total is a record, not a decision.

## Privacy

Neither applicant is named in this repository. The only bracketed field in each
reply is the first name, which the chief executive types into the LinkedIn
message box and never into a committed file. Reply dates, materials and the slot
chosen go to the company's private applicant log, which is kept for four years
and never committed here.
