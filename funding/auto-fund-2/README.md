**A constant stream of funding actions for CEO Kevin Kawchak to make final
decisions on, continued.** The first block, [`../auto-fund`](../auto-fund),
converted the FDA approval of Rasonque into five days of asks. This second block
spends four new signals that arrived in the week of September 21, 2026. Each
business day directory holds everything that day's actions need: verified
recipient addresses, subject lines, complete letter bodies, LinkedIn replies,
technical briefs, field-by-field form content, capital instructions, and one
compiled packet a recipient can be sent. Nothing here is sent, filed, ordered, or
agreed. Each day ends in one approval step, and that step belongs to the chief
executive.

## The standing fact every day repeats

On August 26, 2026 the FDA approved **Rasonque (daraxonrasib)**, Revolution
Medicines' RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, as a first-in-class targeted
therapy for metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma after prior systemic therapy
([FDA press announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-class-targeted-therapy-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer)).
The approval rests on the Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial
([NCT06625320](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320)), which followed the
developer's Phase 1/2 program, registered as the Phase 1/1b study of RMC-6236
([NCT05379985](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05379985)).

ChemicalQDevice's AI papers were developed **independently and simultaneously**
with both of those trial phases. The company selected daraxonrasib from forty
pancreatic cancer meta-analyses in June 2025
([10.5281/zenodo.15735068](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15735068)), simulated a
2.4-fold median overall survival gain in August 2025
([10.5281/zenodo.17001137](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17001137)), and then
built a Phase 1 protocol, an investigational new drug application, a Phase 2
protocol and two funding applications around the molecule while its developer's
own trials were still running. RASolute 302 later reported a 2.0-fold gain. No
relationship of any kind exists with the developer; the papers were written from
public sources, on their own schedule, and every one is deposited with a date.

| Period | Revolution Medicines, public record | ChemicalQDevice, deposited record |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 2022 onward | Phase 1/1b RMC-6236 study opens in RAS-mutant solid tumors | No work on the molecule |
| June 2025 | Phase 3 RASolute 302 enrolling | Gemini ranks a daraxonrasib combination first of forty meta-analyses |
| August 2025 | Phase 3 enrolling | Ten-arm QSP simulation: 12.8 against 5.4 months median overall survival |
| June to August 2026 | RASolute 302 reported; FDA approval on August 26 | Phase 1 protocol, IND, Phase 2 protocol, two funding applications, capitalization plan |

|:--|:--|:--|:--|

| `briefs/` | `.md` | Lightly formatted technical text and tables, with no LaTeX furniture | Technical reviewers who read plain text and repository files |

| `investing/` | `.md` | Instruments, order types, limits, sizes, and the rules that govern the company's own reserve | The chief executive and the brokerage |

| `packet/` | `.tex`, `.sty`, `.bib`, `.pdf`, `.zip` | The compiled document of the day, seven sections, ready for Overleaf | Any recipient who receives an attachment that day |

The format rule is fixed. Anything a person receives as correspondence is `.txt`.
Anything a technical reader reads inline is `.md`. Anything compiled into a PDF is
`.tex` inside a zip for Overleaf.

## The address rule

Every letter in every `emails/` directory carries **at least three recipient
addresses** across its `TO` and `CC` lines, and every one of those addresses was
verified against the publishing organization's own page before it was written
into a letter. No address is bracketed, guessed, or composed from a pattern.
Where an organization publishes no usable address, it is not written to by email:
the White House is reached through its contact form, a brokerage through its
secure portal, and the two applicants through the LinkedIn conversation they
opened. The full register is in [`inputs/README.md`](inputs/README.md).

## The paste rule

Every letter body is written one paragraph per physical line, so that GitHub
shows it unwrapped and a copy into iOS Mail on an iPhone needs no repair. Lines
that are meant to be their own line stay their own line: the salutation,
numbered and bulleted items, and the signature block. No letter body contains a
markdown marker, a tab-aligned table, or a hard break inside a sentence. The
attachment manifests and pre-send checklists below `=== END BODY ===` are the
author's reference material rather than pasted text.

## Compile record

Every packet was compiled with pdfLaTeX and BibTeX before its commit, in the order
`pdflatex` then `bibtex` then `pdflatex` then `pdflatex`. The measured result for
each day, including page count, error count and overfull box count, is recorded
in that day's `packet/README.md`.

## Positioning constraints

Nothing in this directory is a submission of record and nothing here is an
agreement. Each day is a proposal to the chief executive and requires his
approval before any part of it leaves the repository. No order in `investing/`
has been placed and none is investment advice.

The SEC office's words are quoted exactly as received, "very seriously," and are
never described as a finding, a listing, an endorsement, or a decision. The
statement that the company's LLM papers have been used extensively by OpenAI and
Anthropic is the company's own statement, made as the basis of its request; no
agreement, sponsorship, or endorsement exists with either company. The White
House communications are facts about correspondence and are never described as
an award, an appointment, or a commitment. The NSF inquiry is an invitation to
consider applying, and the pilot's published eligibility gate is stated plainly
wherever the pilot is named.

No agreement of any kind exists with Revolution Medicines, UC San Diego, Moores
Cancer Center, or any other institution. Rasonque is approved in the metastatic
setting, and the perioperative use this program proposes remains
investigational. No patient has been treated and no robotic configuration has
been cleared. The two applicants are not named anywhere in this public
repository.
