# The Early-Rise Weekday

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
One page. For the chief executive and every future team member.

---

## Why the day starts at 5:00 a.m.

Washington is three hours ahead of La Jolla. A letter sent at 6:00 a.m. Pacific
arrives at 9:00 a.m. Eastern, at the top of a federal working day, and a reply
that arrives by 2:00 p.m. Pacific can still be answered before the federal day
ends. An early start is the schedule that puts a San Diego company inside the
working hours of the offices it depends on.

## The weekday, block by block

| Pacific time | Eastern time | Block | What happens |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 5:00 to 5:30 | 8:00 to 8:30 | Start | Read overnight replies; no answers yet |
| 5:30 to 6:00 | 8:30 to 9:00 | Approval | The chief executive approves or holds the day's actions |
| 6:00 to 8:00 | 9:00 to 11:00 | Federal window | Letters to federal offices go out; replies are answered |
| 8:00 to 11:00 | 11:00 to 2:00 | Technical work | Protocols, code, packets, and verification |
| 11:00 to 12:00 | 2:00 to 3:00 | West Coast window | Calls and letters to California institutions |
| 12:00 to 12:30 | 3:00 to 3:30 | Break | |
| 12:30 to 2:00 | 3:30 to 5:00 | Second federal window | Same-day answers before Washington closes |
| 2:00 to 3:30 | 5:00 to 6:30 | Build and commit | Everything written today is committed and pushed |
| 3:30 to 4:00 | 6:30 to 7:00 | Record | The day's record is filed; tomorrow's actions are staged |

The day ends at 4:00 p.m. Pacific. Eleven hours from start to record, with the
technical work in one uninterrupted block in the middle.

## The weekly themes inside the timetable

The themes carried from the first block's weekly cadence fill the two federal
windows. The rest of the timetable is the same every day.

| Day | Theme of the federal windows |
|:--|:--|
| Monday | Federal program offices and registrations |
| Tuesday | Regulators, capital, and counsel |
| Wednesday | Sites, partners, and the structures the company works inside |
| Thursday | Pilots, solicitations, and applications |
| Friday | Follow-ups that have earned their interval, and the week's record |

## The team rules

1. Every team member keeps the same start, the same windows, and the same record time.
2. Nothing leaves the company before the 5:30 approval block has passed it.
3. No follow-up is sent inside three business days of the letter it follows, counted in days the recipient's office is open.
4. A reply received after 2:00 p.m. Pacific is answered the next morning in the federal window, not in the evening.
5. The record block is never skipped, because a letter with no record is a letter nobody can reconstruct three weeks later.
