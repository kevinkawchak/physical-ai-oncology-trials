# The Structure Map: Who Governs What

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
For anyone asking which authority governs which part of the program. About 700
words.

---

## Why a map

The second priority is adaptation to existing authority, order, and structure.
A company cannot adapt to a structure it has not drawn. This brief names each
authority the program touches, what that authority governs, and the place the
company takes inside it. In every row the company's place is smaller than the
authority's, and that is the point.

## The map

| Authority | What it governs in this program | The company's place |
|:--|:--|:--|
| The President, through OSTP | The federal science and technology agenda, including AI for science | A correspondent, offering to serve where directed |
| Department of Energy, Genesis Mission | The national AI-for-science platform and its laboratory-led teams | A prospective subcontributor, never a lead |

## Three rules the map implies

**Every investigator decision sits with the site.** Dose assignment within the
escalation rule, dose-limiting toxicity adjudication, and the analysis plan are
held by roles at the site. The chief executive holds 100 percent of the sponsor
and therefore holds none of them. The only decision the company keeps is the
regulatory signature, which a sponsor cannot delegate.

**No authority is asked to do another's work.** The SEC is not asked to judge
clinical research; the FDA is. CTEP is not asked to fund a company concept; it
funds investigators. The letters this block sends are addressed by that rule.

**The company's place is stated in writing before it is taken.** Each letter to
a structure says, in its own words, which place the company is offering to take,
so that no structure has to infer it.

## What the map is not

It is not an organization chart of anything the company belongs to. No
agreement of any kind exists with any authority on the map. It shows where the
company would stand if each structure accepted it, and it shows that in every
case the answer would be "inside, and below."

## How the map changes

A row changes only when an authority answers. When an office replies, the row's
third column is updated to what the office said the company's place is, in the
office's words, and the date of the reply is recorded in the company file.
