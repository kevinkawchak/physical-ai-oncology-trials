## The one fact behind the early start

Washington is three hours ahead. A 6:00 a.m. Pacific letter lands at 9:00 a.m.
Eastern, and a reply received by 2:00 p.m. Pacific can still be answered before
the federal day ends. The early rise is how a San Diego company works inside the
hours of the offices it depends on.
