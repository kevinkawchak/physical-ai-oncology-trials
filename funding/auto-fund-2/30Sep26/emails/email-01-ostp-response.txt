Dear Office of Science and Technology Policy,

On September 12, 2026 I wrote to the President about three priority changes for myself and my startup, and several communications from the White House followed. I have answered through the White House contact form, and I am writing to your office because it is where the federal science and technology agenda is coordinated. I have copied the Department of Energy's Genesis Mission partnerships office and the Office of Personnel Management's AI workforce team, for the reason given below.

The three priorities, as I wrote them, were these.
1) Obedience over hard work and responsibilities.
2) Adapted to existing authority, order, and structure.
3) Acclimated to a teamwork based early-rise, scheduled routine.

A priority is only as good as the rule that follows from it, so I have written each one down as an operating rule for the company.
1) When an office that owns a rule answers a question, the company follows the answer before doing more work of its own.
2) The company takes the role an existing structure offers, even when that role is smaller than the one it asked for.
3) The company's working day begins at 5:00 a.m. Pacific, so that its first correspondence window matches Washington's morning, and every future team member keeps the same schedule.

What the company does. ChemicalQDevice is a one-person San Diego company that develops AI-assisted oncology clinical trial documents. Since June 2025 it has deposited a dated public record around daraxonrasib, developed independently of Revolution Medicines and simultaneously with its Phase 1/2 and Phase 3 trials. The FDA approved the molecule on August 26, 2026 as Rasonque for metastatic pancreatic cancer. The company's Phase 1 protocol proposes a different, investigational, perioperative use.

What I am offering. To serve as a contributor, under direction, inside an established federal structure where AI-assisted clinical research is being organized. The two I can see are the Genesis Mission and the federal AI workforce programs, which is why both offices are copied. I am not asking to lead anything, and I am not asking for funding in this letter.

My one question is which office within the Executive Office of the President, or within an agency, is the right place for a small company to offer that kind of service, so that I can direct any further correspondence there rather than to your office.

Thank you for your time.
