Dear Genesis Mission Partnerships Team,

I am writing to ask whether there is a place in the Genesis Mission for a very small company to serve as a contributor under the direction of a national laboratory or a lead partner, and if so, how that place is found. I have copied the Genesis Mission funding opportunity contact, and the Office of Science and Technology Policy, to which I have written separately.

ChemicalQDevice is a one-person San Diego company. Since June 2025 it has deposited a dated public record of AI-assisted oncology clinical trial work: a drug identification study across forty pancreatic cancer meta-analyses, a quantitative systems pharmacology simulation, a Phase 1 protocol for a robot-assisted pancreatic surgery with a perioperative targeted agent, an investigational new drug application, and the regulatory and site documentation around them. Every item is deposited with a persistent identifier. The agent, daraxonrasib, was approved by the FDA on August 26, 2026 as Rasonque for metastatic pancreatic cancer; the company's work was developed independently of its developer and simultaneously with the developer's trials.

What the company could contribute is narrow and concrete: governed, advisory-only language model workflows for clinical trial documentation, with verification by independent models and a public audit trail. What it could not do is lead a team, operate a laboratory, or hold a large award, and I am not proposing any of those.

Three questions.
1) Does the Genesis Mission have a route by which a small business can join a laboratory-led or partner-led team as a subcontributor, rather than as a lead applicant?
2) Is biomedical and clinical research within the scope of the collaboration agreements, or should I look elsewhere in the Department?
3) If a route exists, is there a point of contact or an event where teams seeking contributors can be found?

The company works to a fixed early-start weekday schedule and takes direction from whichever structure it joins. If the honest answer is that there is no place for a company of this size, that is a useful answer, and I will not follow up on it.

Thank you for considering it.
