## Why the White House itself is not on this list

The White House publishes a contact form rather than an address. Its answer is
in [`../forms/form-01-white-house-contact.md`](../forms/form-01-white-house-contact.md),
and it is submitted first so that letter 1 can say so.

## Why letter 1 copies the Department of Energy and OPM

OSTP coordinates the federal science and technology agenda. The two places in
that agenda where a small AI company could serve under direction are the
Genesis Mission, which the Department of Energy leads, and the federal AI
workforce programs, which the Office of Personnel Management runs. Copying both
tells OSTP where the company proposes to fit, and tells the two offices that the
proposal was made to OSTP first.

## How to send one

1. Open the `.txt` file on GitHub, select everything between `=== BODY ===` and `=== END BODY ===`, and copy it.
2. Paste into a new message in iOS Mail. Each paragraph is one line in the file, so the phone wraps it to the screen with no stray breaks.
3. Copy the `TO`, `CC` and `SUBJECT` lines into their fields separately.
4. Attach the files named under `ATTACHMENTS COMPILED FROM THIS DIRECTORY`.
5. Work through `BEFORE SENDING` line by line.

Send in the early window of the weekday in
[`../briefs/brief-03-the-early-rise-weekday.md`](../briefs/brief-03-the-early-rise-weekday.md):
6:00 to 8:00 a.m. Pacific is 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. in Washington.
