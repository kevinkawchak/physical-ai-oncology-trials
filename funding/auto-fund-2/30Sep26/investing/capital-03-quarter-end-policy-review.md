# Capital Instruction 03: The Quarter-End Policy Review

**ChemicalQDevice LLC corporate brokerage account.** Direction of the sole member
and chief executive. Not investment advice, not a recommendation to any other
person, and not an offer or solicitation. Nothing below is an order unless a
check below requires one.

---

## Why this day

September 30 closes the calendar quarter and the federal fiscal year. The
company's reserve is run by written rules set in the first block, and this
instruction applies the day's second priority, adaptation to existing authority,
order, and structure, to the company's own money: the review checks the account
against the rules, and changes nothing the rules do not require.

## The five checks

| # | Check | Rule it applies | If the check fails |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Every holding is a direct Treasury obligation, the one short Treasury fund, or the government sweep | The ladder's constraints, from the first block | Sell the nonconforming holding at the next open session, with a limit order, and record why it was held |
| 2 | Any rung that matured this quarter has been rolled to the longest rung, about 12 months | The ladder's rolling rule | Enter a non-competitive bid at the next 52-week bill auction for the matured par |
| 3 | Rung A is designated as the payroll earmark and holds at least its ceiling | Capital instruction 01 | Top it up at the next 13-week bill auction, as instruction 01 directs |
| 4 | No restricted security is held, and the restricted list is current | Capital instruction 02 | Stop; take advice before any trade |
| 5 | No federal award money is in the account | The funds-separation rule | Move it to the award account the same day and record the error |

## The one order this instruction can produce

| # | Line | Instrument | Size | Order type | When |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Roll any matured rung | 52-week U.S. Treasury bill | The matured par, rounded to the next $100 | Non-competitive bid at auction | The next 52-week bill auction, which is held every four weeks |

If no rung matured this quarter, no order is entered and the review is recorded
as complete with no action.

## The exception rule

A holding or trade that departs from the written rules requires a written
exception before it is made: one paragraph, dated, stating the rule, the reason,
and the date the exception ends. No exception has been made this quarter, and
this instruction makes none.

## The record

| Item | Entry |
|:--|:--|
| Company file | The five checks, each marked pass or fail, with the date |
| Holdings snapshot | The CUSIP, par, maturity and value of each rung, as of the quarter's last session |
| Orders | Any order entered under line 1, with its auction date and settlement date |
| Next review | The last business day of December |

| Item | Note |
|:--|:--|
