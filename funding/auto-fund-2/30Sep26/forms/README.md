One form pack, submitted before anything else on day 3. It is how the company
answers the White House communications that followed the September 12 message,
because the White House publishes a form rather than an address.

## The pack

| # | File | Portal | When | What it carries |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | [`form-01-white-house-contact.md`](form-01-white-house-contact.md) | whitehouse.gov contact form | First, before letter 1 | Every field's answer, and a 1,212-character message pasted as one block |

## Why it is submitted before the OSTP letter

Letter 1 to the Office of Science and Technology Policy opens by saying the
company has answered through the White House contact form. The order makes that
sentence true when it is read, which is the first of the three operating rules
applied to the company's own correspondence.

## What the message does and does not do

| It does | It does not |
|:--|:--|
| Thank the White House for its communications | Quote or characterize those communications |
| Restate the three priorities in the September 12 wording | Change the wording, even slightly |
| State the operating rule each priority became | Ask for funding, a meeting, or an appointment |
| Say where the company has offered to serve | Describe any communication as an award or endorsement |
