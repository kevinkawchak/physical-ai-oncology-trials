\section{The Early-Rise Weekday}

The third priority is a teamwork-based early-rise, scheduled routine. The
reason for the early start is practical. Washington is three hours ahead of La
Jolla, so a letter sent at 6:00 a.m. Pacific arrives at 9:00 a.m. Eastern, at the
top of a federal working day, and a reply received by 2:00 p.m. Pacific can still
be answered before that day ends. Table 14 sets out the day block by block, in
both time zones.

\subsection{The five team rules}

Every team member keeps the same start, the same windows, and the same record
time. Nothing leaves the company before the 5:30 approval block has passed it. No
follow-up is sent inside three business days of the letter it follows, counted in
days the recipient's office is open. A reply received after 2:00 p.m. Pacific is
answered the next morning in the federal window, not in the evening. And the
record block is never skipped, because a letter with no record is a letter
nobody can reconstruct three weeks later.

\subsection{The team the routine is for}

The company has one member today and two unsolicited applicants, and each reply
on day 1 told the applicant about the schedule before anything else about the
role. The routine is written for a team before there is one, so that the first
people to join are joining a structure rather than building it.
