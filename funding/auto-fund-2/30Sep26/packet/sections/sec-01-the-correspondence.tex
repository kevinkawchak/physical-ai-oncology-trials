\section{The Message, and the Three Rules It Became}

\subsection{The message, quoted as sent}

\begin{quote}\small
Dear Mr. Trump,\\[0.4em]

for myself and the startup which include:\\
1) Obedience over hard work and responsibilities.\\
2) Adapted to existing authority, order, and structure.\\
3) Acclimated to a teamwork based early-rise, scheduled routine.\\[0.4em]

CEO Kevin Kawchak\\
ChemicalQDevice\\
September 12, 2026
\end{quote}

Several communications from the White House followed. Their text is not in the
repository, and this packet neither quotes nor paraphrases them. They are facts
about correspondence: not an award, an appointment, an endorsement, or a
commitment, and nothing here describes them as one.

\subsection{Why each priority needs a rule}

A priority stated in a letter is a promise, and a promise a counterparty cannot
check is worth what the counterparty chooses to believe. A small company cannot
afford to depend on belief, so each priority is restated in Table 11 as a rule,
beside the behavior that shows the rule is being kept.

\subsection{The first rule, already kept once}

The first block of funding actions wrote to the National Cancer Institute's
Cancer Therapy Evaluation Program as a company bringing a concept. The branch
chief answered that the program's trials are investigator-initiated, so a
concept that originates with a company is the wrong shape by construction
\cite{ctep2026,autofund2026}. The company did not argue. It rewrote its approach
around the structure it had been shown, and day 3 of this block carries that
rewrite to the academic cancer center that would hold the trial. That is the
first rule, kept before it was written down.

\subsection{The standing fact}

The trial those structures would hold is built around daraxonrasib, which the
FDA approved on August 26, 2026 as Rasonque for metastatic pancreatic
adenocarcinoma after prior systemic therapy
\cite{fdarasonque2026,fdadisco2026,revmedapproval2026}. The approval followed
Revolution Medicines' Phase 1/2 program and its Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial
\cite{ctgov001,ctgov302,rasolute302}. ChemicalQDevice's AI papers on the molecule
were developed independently of that program and simultaneously with it, from
the June 2025 identification study \cite{daraxident} to the Phase 1 protocol and
investigational new drug application of 2026 \cite{onpremwhippl,phase1ind}. The
perioperative use the company's protocol proposes remains investigational.
