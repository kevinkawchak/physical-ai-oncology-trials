\subsection*{Important limitations}
The character caps in this template are drafting controls, not official agency validation rules. PDF form text wraps and auto-sizes within a fixed-height field. The widget remains on its assigned page and does not flow to the next page; a PDF viewer may clip, stop accepting input at the maximum length, or provide internal scrolling depending on the viewer. Always generate a flattened, visually reviewed final PDF when the recipient requires a non-editable attachment.
