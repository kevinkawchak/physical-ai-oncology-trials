\GrantTextArea[
  id=facilities-clinical-resources, font=10pt, height=1.05in, maxchars=2300
]{Clinical sites, subject populations, laboratories, pharmacies, imaging, testing, and specimen resources}{Comprehensive documentation for establishing the first California Physical AI oncology clinical trial site in San Francisco. These 11 documents collectively provide legislation drafts, regulatory updates, and building and premises code standards required for site authorization and operation. 
A legal framework is provided for authorizing Physical AI oncology clinical trial sites in California. This legislation draft creates^^Ja standardized authorization process through the California Department of Public Health, defines Physical AI system categories and scoring requirements, and establishes safety and reporting.
}
\GrantTextArea[
  id=facilities-data-resources, font=10pt, height=0.95in, maxchars=1900
]{Data coordinating, biostatistics, informatics, EDC, cybersecurity, and records infrastructure}{The project will use a centralized data-coordination model linking EDC, source-document control, safety reporting, statistical programming, repository versioning, and records retention. Biostatistics support will oversee the SAP, cohort-review outputs, exact-interval estimation, decision-gate tracking, and final analysis sets. Infrastructure will include repositories, structured metadata, file naming, audit logging, and environments. \citep{phase1guidance,oncotrialllm,natlmcpservr,fedlearnpaio}.
}
\GrantTextArea[
  id=facilities-collaboration, font=10pt, height=1.1in, maxchars=1700
]{Collaborative arrangements and access to specialized capabilities}{No external collaborator should be represented as committed unless documented at submission. The project is designed to operate through prospective agreements with a surgical site, medical oncology and anesthesia teams, CLIA-qualified molecular testing, pathology and imaging cores, investigational pharmacy, medical monitoring, external biostatistics support, and device/software specialists. ChemicalQDevice's contribution is concept integration, sponsor-level coordination, repo-based documentation, and governance \citep{phase1ind,onpremwhippl,paisitedocpk,natlpaiplatf}.
