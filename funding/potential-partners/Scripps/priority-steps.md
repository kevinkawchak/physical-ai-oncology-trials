14. Frame the pilot-funding concept around the AI method.

Use this prospective pilot title:

A Generalizable Verification and Provenance Framework for LLM-Assisted Clinical-Trial Development

Do not use this as the pilot title:

Daraxonrasib Robotic Whipple Clinical Trial

The pilot should test questions such as:

Can independent AI-model review reliably identify unsupported clinical and regulatory claims?

Can repository and source provenance reduce document-development errors?

Can human reviewers reproduce the reasoning behind AI-generated trial materials?

Which disagreements among AI models predict genuine document defects?

Can standardized assurance gates be transferred across different diseases and trial types?

The pancreatic cancer materials can serve as a use case, but the result should be generalizable beyond pancreatic cancer.
