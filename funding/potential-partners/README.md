# UC San Diego and Scripps Clinical Research Potential Partnerships


## UC San Diego Clinical Trial Partnership Overview

### Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of a potential UC San Diego pathway for advancing a verification-first AI oncology program toward institutional review, funding, and a future pancreatic cancer clinical trial.

The central objective is to determine whether UC San Diego has the clinical, surgical, regulatory, operational, and technical capacity to evaluate and potentially host the proposed research.

### Strategic Focus

UC San Diego should be approached as a direct clinical-development partner. The initial work should focus on feasibility rather than promotion.

Priority areas include:

- Pancreatic surgical oncology review.
- Gastrointestinal medical oncology review.
- Clinical-trial operations and budgeting.
- Robotic surgery and workflow feasibility.
- AI validation, cybersecurity, and human oversight.
- Regulatory planning for the drug, software, and surgical components.
- Institutional contracting, IRB review, monitoring, and data management.
- NIH, SBIR, philanthropic, and local funding opportunities.

### Recommended Sequence

1. Prepare a concise, nonconfidential scientific overview.
2. Define the exact patient population, intervention, robotic platform, and AI role.
3. Request a joint feasibility meeting with pancreatic surgery, medical oncology, and clinical-trial operations.
4. Confirm whether a qualified UC San Diego investigator is willing to lead or sponsor the study.
5. Obtain a preliminary assessment of eligible-patient volume, competing trials, operating-room capacity, pharmacy support, and expected enrollment.
6. Submit the appropriate investigator-initiated trial intake and budget materials.
7. Engage institutional regulatory, contracting, IRB, coverage-analysis, and research-administration teams.
8. Conduct a technical review of the robotic and AI workflow.
9. Clarify FDA requirements and determine whether an IND, IDE, or combination-product pathway applies.
10. Define the legal sponsor, regulatory holder, monitoring, safety, insurance, data, and intellectual-property responsibilities.
11. Secure manufacturer cooperation for investigational drug access and regulatory cross-reference.
12. Pursue federal, small-business, philanthropic, and San Diego funding in parallel.

### Required Positioning

The AI system should be described as bounded and advisory. Licensed clinicians must retain final authority over diagnosis, treatment, surgery, and safety decisions.

The proposal should clearly distinguish:

- Simulation results.
- Draft protocol concepts.
- Unvalidated software.
- Proposed clinical research.
- Established clinical evidence.

UC San Diego should not be publicly described as a partner, sponsor, trial site, or endorser unless written authorization is obtained.

### Success Criteria

A productive early outcome may include:

- An invitation to present the concept.
- Identification of a clinical principal investigator.
- Acceptance into an institutional feasibility process.
- A preliminary budget and accrual estimate.
- A technical review of the robotic and AI workflow.
- A defined regulatory and contracting pathway.
- Support for a grant or pilot application.

A patient-facing trial should proceed only after the site, manufacturer, FDA pathway, funding, sponsor responsibilities, contracts, insurance, IRB approval, and operational resources are documented.

### Working Goal

Use UC San Diego to determine whether the program is clinically credible, operationally feasible, appropriately regulated, and fundable as a staged translational research effort.

## Scripps AI and Clinical Research Partnership Overview

### Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of a potential Scripps pathway for advancing a verification-first AI oncology program toward independent evaluation, pilot funding, translational collaboration, and a future pancreatic cancer clinical application.

The central objective is to create credible institutional interest in the AI work before requesting clinical-trial hosting.

### Strategic Focus

Scripps should be approached through an AI-first and digital-trials strategy.

Priority areas include:

- Independent review of the public AI oncology portfolio.
- Verification, provenance, reproducibility, and auditability.
- Human oversight and model-disagreement analysis.
- Digital clinical-trial methods.
- AI-assisted protocol and regulatory-document development.
- Human-factors and workflow research.
- Pilot studies with generalizable translational value.
- Later introductions to clinical research, pancreatic oncology, and surgical teams.
- Pilot, SBIR, philanthropic, and commercialization funding.

### Recommended Sequence

1. Publish a concise, credible public AI oncology portfolio.
2. Clearly identify methods, source provenance, human review, limitations, and regulatory status.
3. Create a short institutional review brief focused on verification-first AI and digital-trial methods.
4. Obtain independent technical or scientific review of at least part of the work.
5. Send one informational notice inviting Scripps researchers to evaluate the portfolio.
6. Allow Scripps research leadership to route the work internally.
7. Request an initial meeting focused on AI validation and a limited pilot.
8. Define a generalizable pilot around provenance, model disagreement, human review, or clinical-trial document quality.
9. Introduce the pancreatic cancer program only after interest in the AI framework is established.
10. Request introductions to clinical research operations, pancreatic medical oncology, surgical oncology, biostatistics, regulatory affairs, and contracting.
11. Conduct institutional feasibility review for a staged clinical application.
12. Clarify manufacturer, FDA, sponsor, insurance, monitoring, data, and intellectual-property responsibilities.
13. Pursue Scripps pilot funding, SBIR support, philanthropy, and local San Diego commercialization resources.

### Required Positioning

The AI work should be presented as an investigational research portfolio, not as validated clinical decision-making software.

The communication should emphasize that:

- The AI does not hold clinical authority.
- Human experts remain responsible for all conclusions.
- Simulation is not clinical evidence.
- Public documents are available for independent evaluation.
- A future pancreatic cancer study is one possible application, not the sole value of the work.

Scripps should not be publicly described as a partner, sponsor, trial site, or endorser unless written authorization is obtained.

### Success Criteria

A productive early outcome may include:

- A request for additional AI materials.
- An invitation to present the portfolio.
- An introduction to an AI or digital-trials investigator.
- Independent validation of part of the work.
- An invitation to prepare a pilot concept.
- An introduction to clinical research operations.
- A staged plan for retrospective, observational, or interventional research.

A patient-facing trial should be considered only after institutional investigators, clinical operations, the drug manufacturer, FDA, funding sources, and legal sponsors confirm feasibility and responsibility.

### Working Goal

Use Scripps to establish independent credibility for the AI methods, develop a focused translational pilot, and create a responsible pathway toward a later clinical application.
