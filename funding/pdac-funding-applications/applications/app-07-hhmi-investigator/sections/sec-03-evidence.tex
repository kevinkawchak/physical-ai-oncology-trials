\section{The Record So Far}

Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) is a RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor. RASolute 302
reported median overall survival of \textbf{13.2 months} against \textbf{6.6}
for chemotherapy in the RAS G12 population of previously treated metastatic
pancreatic cancer \cite{rasolute302}. Nine months earlier, a ten-arm
quantitative systems pharmacology simulation with 250 ordinary differential
equations per patient had returned \textbf{12.8} against \textbf{5.4 months}
\cite{qspmetpancre}. The proximity is chronology and hypothesis support, not
validation: 1000 simulated against 241 enrolled, a combination against a single
agent, KRAS G12C against a primarily G12D and G12V population.
