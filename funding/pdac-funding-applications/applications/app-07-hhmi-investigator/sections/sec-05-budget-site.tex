\section{Seven Years, and the Partner Site}

The report's reference point for person-based support is roughly \$10 million
over seven years. This programme's clinical costs are lower than a bench
laboratory's: \textbf{\$700,000 per year} covers the trial and its verification
work \cite{fundapp2}, and the remainder of a standard investigator allocation
would fund the pharmacologic and surrogate work that follows the Phase 1 readout.
