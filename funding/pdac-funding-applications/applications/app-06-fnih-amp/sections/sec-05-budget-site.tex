\section{Cost Share, Leverage, and the Partner Site}

The federal or foundation ask is \textbf{\$700,000 per year for five years},
\$3,500,000 total \cite{fundapp2}. The consortium's contributed value, drug
supply, site infrastructure, pathology, and bioanalytical support, is not
requested in cash and is the non-federal share the annex asks agencies to
prioritise \cite{kratsiosvought2026fy2028priorities}.
