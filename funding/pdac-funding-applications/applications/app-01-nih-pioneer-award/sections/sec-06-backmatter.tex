\bmhead{Scope of Claims}
\vspace{-0.2em}

Claimed: fourteen citable works, three verified simulations, and a complete
regulatory package produced by one scientist in fourteen months. Not claimed:
that the simulations validate RASolute 302, that the advisory system holds any
clearance, that anything here has been used in a human, or that UC San Diego has
agreed to anything. The trial does not exist until a site, an IRB, an active
IND, monitoring, insurance, and a sponsor of record are in place.

\bmhead{Availability and Conflicts}
\vspace{-0.2em}

Every work cited to a DOI is openly deposited; code, verification notebooks, and
figure sources are in the repository \cite{repo2026}. The applicant holds no
equity in Revolution Medicines or any robotic surgery vendor, and no sponsor,
contract research organization, or medical society reviewed this document.
