\section{Budget, Dissolution, and the Partner Site}

The request is \textbf{\$700,000 per year for five years}, \$3,500,000 total,
with no cost share and no sixth year \cite{fundapp2}. The dissolution provision
is budgeted rather than assumed: year five carries the archival, handoff, and
closure costs explicitly.
