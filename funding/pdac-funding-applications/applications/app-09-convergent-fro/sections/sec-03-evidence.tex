\section{The Gap the Dataset Fills}

Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) has a metastatic readout. RASolute 302 reported median
overall survival of \textbf{13.2 months} against \textbf{6.6} for chemotherapy
in the RAS G12 population of previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer
\cite{rasolute302}. A ten-arm quantitative systems pharmacology simulation had
returned \textbf{12.8} against \textbf{5.4 months} nine months earlier
\cite{qspmetpancre}; that proximity is chronology, not validation, and the three
differences are sample size 1000 against 241, a combination against a single
agent, and KRAS G12C against a primarily G12D and G12V population.
