\bmhead{Scope of Claims and Positioning}
\vspace{-0.2em}

Claimed: a published Phase 1 protocol, a published Investigational New Drug
application, a fixed statistical and safety plan, and an audited simulation
package. Not claimed: that daraxonrasib is first-in-human, that any robotic
configuration has been specified or cleared for the described use, that any
drug supply or letter of authorization exists, or that UC San Diego has agreed
to anything at all. No patient has been treated.

UC San Diego and Moores Cancer Center are not described in this document, in the
covering email, or in any other application in this set as a partner, sponsor,
trial site, or endorser, and will not be so described without written
authorization. Simulation results, draft protocol concepts, unvalidated
software, proposed clinical research, and established clinical evidence are
labelled separately wherever they appear.
