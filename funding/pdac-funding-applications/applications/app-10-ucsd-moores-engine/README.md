The tenth application is addressed to the **partner institution itself** rather
than to an external funder, and it asks for a feasibility review only. Dated
**August 3, 2026**.

## Why this one is different

Every other application names UC San Diego as the intended partner. This one
asks UC San Diego whether that is even possible. It follows the sequence, the
required positioning, and the success criteria recorded in
[`../../../potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/priority-steps.md`](../../../potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/priority-steps.md)
and [`README.md`](../../../potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/README.md), including
the recipient routing and the three-business-day escalation path.

## Three positioning corrections carried from the author's own source

| Correction | Why |
|:--|:--|
| Daraxonrasib is **not** first-in-human | It is investigational and already in Phase 3. The supportable claim is the first prospective clinical evaluation of the integrated surgical and LLM advisory workflow |
| The robotic configuration is **not** specified | Manufacturer, model, cleared configuration, arm count, instruments, software version, and intended use are an agenda item, not an assertion |
| No drug supply or cross-reference exists | Stated plainly in the email, the attachment, and the back matter |

## Figures, and why each type was chosen

| Fig | Type | Why this platform |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | mermaid-type sequence | The subject is **an institutional route between offices**. Only a sequence diagram puts the sponsor-investigator determination visibly before the IRB submission |
| 2 | plantuml-type use case | The subject is **who holds which clinical decision right**. Associations state it, and the struck link states that the applicant holds none |
| 3 | d2-type feasibility grid | The subject is **five questions, each with an owner and a consequence**. A grid answers all three columns at once |
| 4 | graphviz-type approval dependency | The subject is **what must precede what**, with exactly one edge that cannot be relaxed |
