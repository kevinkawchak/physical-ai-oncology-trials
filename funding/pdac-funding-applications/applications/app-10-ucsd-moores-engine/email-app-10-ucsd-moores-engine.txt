Three corrections before anything else, because each would otherwise mislead.
Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) is not first-in-human; it is investigational and already

subject to FDA and institutional confirmation. The robotic configuration must be

software version, and intended use before feasibility or regulatory status can
be assessed; specifying it is an agenda item, not a claim I am making. And no
drug supply, letter of authorization, or regulatory cross-reference is in place
with the agent's developer.

The concept is a Phase 1, open-label, single-arm 3+3 dose escalation of
perioperative daraxonrasib in up to 18 participants with resectable or
borderline-resectable KRAS G12-mutated pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. The
language model is confined to advisory output: no generated token reaches a
motion controller, and the operating surgeon approves every motion.

Five questions I would like the meeting to settle:

1. Is the patient population and the perioperative treatment concept clinically

2. Could a qualified pancreatic surgeon serve as clinical site PI, and could a

3. What is the annual eligible-patient count, and what is operating-room and
   pharmacy capacity for a research case?
4. Is the proposed robotic configuration available and clinically permissible
   here, which I would follow up with the Center for the Future of Surgery?
5. Is the institution willing in principle to host an investigator-initiated

honest starting point for this discussion.
