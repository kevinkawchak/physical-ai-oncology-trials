Dear ARPA-H Mission Office,

I am submitting a proposal abstract as a single independent performer and asking
which mission office, if any, should receive the full submission.

The program is a 36-month, milestone-bounded Phase 1 study combining perioperative daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) with a staged eight-arm robotic
pancreaticoduodenectomy and an on-premises large language model whose output is
strictly advisory. It is written to be run the way the ARPA model is meant to be
run: three gates, each carrying a numeric kill criterion that a program manager
can check against data the program itself produces, rather than a narrative
progress review.

Gate 1 at month 12 fails if the IND is not active, if measured arm-level stop
latency exceeds 3 milliseconds, or if system-wide stop exceeds 500 milliseconds.
Gate 2 at month 21 fails on any unexpected grade 4 event attributed to the device
or the advisory path in the first cohort. Gate 3 at month 33 fails if fewer than
two evaluable participants are accrued at any dose level, or if no dose level is
declared tolerable.

Much of what would normally consume an ARPA program's first eighteen months is
already finished and published. A Phase 1 protocol, a Phase 2 protocol, an
Investigational New Drug application, and four guidance works exist and carry
DOIs. Three quantitative pancreatic cancer trial simulations were completed at
roughly $36,330 each against an industry benchmark of $120,000 to $2,000,000 per
run, and the digital-twin work carries an ASME V&V 40 and ICH M15 aligned
credibility score of 81.9 across 55 verification notebook tests.

The request is $2,100,000 over 36 months, $700,000 per year, with no cost share.
The transition out of the program is an RP2D, a public safety set, and an open
verification package any qualified site can reuse. I intend to partner at UC San
Diego Moores Cancer Center at the feasibility stage only; UC San Diego is not a
sponsor, site, or endorser of this work.

The attached five-page abstract carries the gate schedule, the authority
boundaries, the surgical measures, and the budget by layer. I would appreciate
direction on the appropriate mission office and on whether a solo performer
