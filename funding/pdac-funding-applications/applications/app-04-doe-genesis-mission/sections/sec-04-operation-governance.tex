\section{Gold Standard Science, Artifact by Artifact}

Executive Order 14303 sets nine principles for federally funded research
\cite{eo14303goldstandard}. Rather than assert compliance, this program names
the artifact that discharges each principle and the field a reviewer reads.

\begin{appfloat}[!tb]
\begin{appfig}[graphviz-type / record nodes]
