\section{The Robotics Mission Names This Work}

The FY 2028 budget priorities memorandum annexed to \textit{Science: A New
Golden Age} names six national missions \cite{kratsiosvought2026fy2028priorities}.
The Robotics mission is written as ``general-purpose autonomous systems capable
of dexterous manipulation, mobility, and reliable operation in real-world
environments, to initiate the era of physical AI-driven scientific discovery and
American reindustrialization.'' An eight-arm robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy
under a governed advisory model is that sentence applied to a human operating
room, which is the least forgiving real-world environment available.

The same memorandum asks agencies to realign a roughly \textbf{\$200 billion}
annual portfolio toward the individual scientist, to fund AI as a new instrument
of discovery rather than for incremental gains, and to use federal purchasing
power to drive instrument redesign toward open interfaces, standardized data
formats, and cross-vendor interoperability \cite{kratsios2026sciencegoldenage}.
This proposal is written to be the small, hard case that tests all three at once.
