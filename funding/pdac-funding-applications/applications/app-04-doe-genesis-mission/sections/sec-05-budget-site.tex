\section{Mission Alignment, Budget, and the Partner Site}

The request is \textbf{\$700,000 per year for five years}, \$3,500,000 total,
with no cost share \cite{fundapp2}. The operation is a staged eight-arm robotic
pancreaticoduodenectomy with perioperative daraxonrasib in resectable or
borderline-resectable KRAS G12-mutated PDAC, open-label, single-arm, 3+3
escalation, up to 18 treated participants \cite{onpremwhippl,phase1ind}.
Arm-level stop is specified at 3 milliseconds and system-wide stop at 500
milliseconds, measured on the bench and re-measured at each cohort boundary.
