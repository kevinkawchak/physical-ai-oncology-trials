\bmhead{Scope of Claims}
\vspace{-0.2em}

Claimed: a demonstrated cost and schedule per work product roughly one
thirtieth of the industry benchmark, a published protocol and IND, and a
governance package that is site-portable by construction. Not claimed: that the simulations validate
RASolute 302, that the advisory system holds any regulatory clearance, that
anything here has been used in a human, or that UC San Diego has agreed to
anything. The trial does not exist until a site, an IRB, an active IND,
monitoring, insurance, and a sponsor of record are all in place.

\bmhead{Availability and Conflicts}
\vspace{-0.2em}

Every work cited to a DOI is openly deposited; code, verification notebooks, and
figure sources are in the repository \cite{repo2026}. The applicant holds no
equity in Revolution Medicines or any robotic surgery vendor, and no sponsor,
contract research organization, or medical society reviewed this document.
