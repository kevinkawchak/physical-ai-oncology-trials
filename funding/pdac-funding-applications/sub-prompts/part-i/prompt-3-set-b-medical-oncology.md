### Objective

Write the five medical-oncology-perspective application file sets, under the
same contract as Stage 2 and to the same standard. Same applicant, same date of
**August 3, 2026**, same partner of choice, **UC San Diego Moores Cancer
Center**, same hybrid operation carrying both surgical and medical oncology

### What "medical oncology perspective" means here

The lead argument is the drug and the patient selection: daraxonrasib
(RMC-6236), a RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, given perioperatively in KRAS
G12-mutated resectable or borderline-resectable PDAC, with the operation as the
window that makes the pharmacology measurable. The endpoint table is led by
dose-limiting toxicity and the 3+3 escalation, pharmacokinetic and
pharmacodynamic sampling around the resection, pathologic response, ctDNA
clearance, and 24-month overall survival. The robotic operation is described in
full, but as the instrumented setting rather than the headline.

### Evidence emphasis specific to Set B

Every application in this set must carry the numerical chronology that makes the
program credible to a medical oncology reviewer:

- The August 2025 QSP simulation: median overall survival 12.8 months for the
  daraxonrasib combination against 5.4 months for chemotherapy, 250 ODEs per
  patient, ten arms, best mOS hazard ratio 0.25.
- The May 2026 RASolute 302 readout: 13.2 months against 6.6 months in the RAS
  G12 population.
- The explicit statement that the proximity of the two is a chronology
  observation and a hypothesis-supporting one, **not** a validation claim, with
  the three differences named: sample size 1000 against 241; combination against
  single agent; KRAS G12C selection against a primarily G12D and G12V
  population.
- The digital-twin work: best mPFS hazard ratio 0.31, mOS hazard ratio 0.34,
  ASME V&V 40 and FDA M15 aligned credibility score 81.9, 55 verification
  notebook tests.
