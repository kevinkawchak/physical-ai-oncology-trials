One pack rather than two. This day submits rather than registers, and the single
pack is the checklist that gates the submission.

## Why a checklist and not another field pack

The field packs of days 1, 2, 3 and 4 answer portal questions. This one answers a
different question: whether a submission is ready to be made at all. Twenty-four
items, five of which block a submission outright and nineteen of which cost
review score rather than eligibility.

Separating the two classes is the point. An applicant who treats all
twenty-four as equal spends the last week before a receipt date on formatting
while a blocking item stays unresolved.

## What the five blocking items are

Named here as well as in the pack, because a reader who opens only one file
should still see them: an active federal entity registration; a current Company
Registry control number; two separate eRA Commons accounts where one person holds
both roles; an Authorized Organization Representative approval; and a Principal
Investigator whose primary employment is with the small business during the
award.

Each of the five is a yes or no, and a no on any of them means the submission
does not happen regardless of how good the science is.
