# Form Pack 01: SBIR Phase I Submission Checklist

**What this gates.** Whether a Small Business Innovation Research Phase I
application can be submitted at all, and separately, whether it will read well.
**Status.** Checklist run. No application has been submitted.

---

## The two classes of item

| Class | Count | What a failure costs |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Blocking** | 5 | The submission does not happen, regardless of the science |
| Scoring | 19 | Review score, not eligibility |

An applicant who treats all twenty-four as equal spends the last week before a
receipt date on formatting while a blocking item stays unresolved. The five are
therefore checked first, every time, and none of them is a matter of judgment.

## Group A: the five blocking items

| # | Item | Checked against | Status |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| A1 | Federal entity registration is **active**, not expired | The entity record's expiration date | Confirm on the day |
| A2 | Company Registry control number is **current** | The registry record | Confirm on the day |
| A3 | Two separate eRA Commons accounts exist where one person holds both the Signing Official and the Principal Investigator roles | Both account records | Confirm on the day |
| A4 | Authorized Organization Representative approval is in place | Granted by the entity's eBiz point of contact, not by the portal | Confirm on the day |
| A5 | The Principal Investigator's primary employment is with the small business during the award | The effort commitment, honestly assessed | Confirm on the day |

A5 is the one most often answered optimistically. It is a factual question about
where a person's primary employment sits during the award period, and answering
it hopefully creates a problem that surfaces at award rather than at submission.

## Group B: registration and identifiers, five items

| # | Item |
|:--|:--|
| B1 | Unique Entity Identifier matches the entity record character for character |
| B2 | Grants.gov organization registration is complete |
| B3 | Grants.gov workspace exists and every attachment has passed portal validation |
| B4 | ORCID is linked to the Principal Investigator profile, and the linked record is populated |
| B5 | The eRA Commons username is recorded where the application needs it, not recalled from memory |

## Group C: the budget, five items

| # | Item | Value |
|:--|:--|:--|
| C1 | Phase I total cost | $306,000 |
| C2 | Phase I duration | 9 months |
| C3 | Phase II total cost, stated for the follow-on | $1,300,000 |
| C4 | Phase II duration | 24 months |
| C5 | Indirect cost treatment consistent with the applicable allowance, and the fee stated separately from cost | Per the program's own guidance |

C5 is where a first-time small business applicant most often produces an
internally inconsistent budget: the fee is calculated on the sum of direct and
indirect costs and is not itself a cost of the project, and a budget that treats
it as one will not reconcile.

## Group D: the narrative, six items

| # | Item |
|:--|:--|
| D1 | Specific aims match the five Phase I milestones, one for one |
| D2 | Every quantitative claim carries the limitation its own authors stated, in the same sentence |
| D3 | No sentence describes daraxonrasib as first in human |
| D4 | No sentence implies the August 2026 approval covers the perioperative use |
| D5 | No institution is described as a partner, sponsor, site, or endorser |
| D6 | No robotic manufacturer, model, arm count, or software version is asserted |

## Group E: attachments and consistency, three items

| # | Item |
|:--|:--|
| E1 | The biosketch's publication list resolves through the linked ORCID record |
| E2 | Facilities and resources describe what exists, and what would be secured, as two separate things |
| E3 | Every figure in every attachment is legible at print size, and no attachment carries a raster image that degrades |

## What is checked in what order, on the day

The five blocking items first, in five minutes. If any is a no, stop and fix it;
nothing else matters that day. Then group C, because a budget inconsistency is the
next most expensive thing to find late. Then D, then B, then E.

## What this checklist does not do

It does not assess the science, it does not predict a score, and it does not
substitute for reading the funding opportunity announcement, whose own
requirements govern and which is re-read for every receipt date because it
changes.
