# The Weekly Cadence

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
One page. If it does not fit on a page it will not be followed.

---

## Why a cadence at all

A week that produces five days of correspondence and no recurring rhythm is a
week that has to be reinvented. The five-day block just completed was designed
from nothing, which cost more time in design than in execution. The next block
should be a substitution of content into a known frame.

## The frame

| Weekday | Theme | Owner | Time budget |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Monday | Federal: re-contacts, program questions, registrations | CEO | 90 minutes |
| Tuesday | Capital: instruments, counsel, treasury, filings | CEO | 75 minutes |
| Wednesday | Site and partner: institutions, foundations, start-up support | CEO | 90 minutes |
| Thursday | Preparation: data room, diligence answers, next week's drafts | CEO | 120 minutes |
| Friday | Execution and record: release, follow-ups, the week's record table | CEO | 75 minutes |

Seven and a half hours a week, which is what one person can sustain alongside the
technical work that produces the artifacts everything else describes.

## The rules that make it survive contact with a real week

**A holiday moves the theme, it does not delete it.** The block just completed
put its preparation day on a federal holiday, which was the right move and is the
general rule: whichever weekday is closed becomes the preparation day, and the
themes shift around it.

**No day runs over its budget.** A Monday that takes three hours has borrowed
from Thursday, and Thursday is the day that makes the following week possible. If
a theme cannot fit its budget, it is carried rather than expanded.

**Follow-ups obey the three-business-day interval** regardless of what day they
land on. The cadence does not override it, and a Friday follow-up to a Wednesday
letter does not happen.

**Friday's record table is written on Friday.** Not at month end, not
retrospectively. The one page a person who missed the week reads is worth
fifteen minutes while the week is still legible.

## What each day produces, in artifact terms

| Weekday | The artifact that must exist by the end of it |
|:--|:--|
| Monday | Sent letters, and any registration confirmed current |
| Tuesday | A capital decision recorded, or a written reason none was taken |
| Wednesday | Sent letters, and any meeting confirmed with an agenda attached |
| Thursday | The data room updated, and next week's drafts written and held |
| Friday | Orders entered against their checks, follow-ups sent, and the record table filed |

An artifact that does not exist by the end of its day is carried to the same day
next week rather than squeezed into the next one. A cadence that reschedules
within a week is a cadence that stops being a cadence by Wednesday.

## What is deliberately not in the cadence

Fundraising conversations that have no scheduled next action. Reading. Writing
the technical artifacts themselves, which is the rest of the week and is not
funding work. And anything that would be chased rather than waited for: the four
unchaseable items in the pipeline brief are checked on Thursday and never acted
on.

## How the next block is built from this

Take the five weekday themes, substitute the current content, and the design
work is done. The five-day block just completed maps onto the frame exactly:
federal, capital, site and partner, preparation, execution. That was not
accidental, and the mapping is the argument that the cadence is the right one.
