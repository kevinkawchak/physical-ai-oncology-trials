Two packs, completed offline against portals whose support desks are closed.

## Why neither is submitted today

A portal submission that fails on a federal holiday cannot be helped until the
next session, and both of these submissions have a failure mode that requires
help: a registration mismatch that the portal reports as a generic error, and a
profile linkage that fails silently.

Completing them offline today means the next session begins with the typing
already done and only the submission remaining, which is the difference between
twenty minutes and two hours.

| 1 | [`form-01-grants-gov-workspace-setup.md`](form-01-grants-gov-workspace-setup.md) | Grants.gov workspace | A workspace created after a funding opportunity announcement is found is a workspace created under time pressure |

## The dependency both inherit

Both depend on the SAM.gov entity registration from day 1
\[[`../../02Sep26/forms/form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md`](../../02Sep26/forms/form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md)\].
Neither can be completed if that registration has lapsed, and a lapse is silent.
Both packs open with a check on it for that reason.
