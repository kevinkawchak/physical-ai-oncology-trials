# The Data Room: Nine Folders, and the Question Each Answers

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
An internal index. Nothing in this brief is shared with any counterparty until
that counterparty asks for a folder by name.

---

## The principle

A data room organized by document type is a data room nobody can navigate. A data
room organized by **the question each folder answers** is one a reviewer can work
through without a call. Every folder below is named for its question.

## The nine folders

| # | Folder | The question it answers | Access class |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | Entity and ownership | Who is this company, who owns it, and since when | Open on request |
| 02 | The program | What is being built, and what stage is it at | Public already |
| 03 | The evidence | What is known, and with what limitations | Public already |
| 04 | Regulatory | What has been prepared, what has been submitted, and what has not | Open on request |
| 05 | Capital | What has been raised, what is planned, and what the gap is | Open under a confidentiality agreement |
| 06 | Institutional correspondence | Who has been approached, when, and with what result | Open under a confidentiality agreement |
| 07 | Recognition and responses | What correspondence has been received, stated precisely | Open on request |
| 08 | Team and roles | Who does the work today, and who would be hired | Open on request |
| 09 | Risks and stop conditions | What would make this program stop | Open on request |

Folders 02 and 03 are already public in full, which shortens most diligence
processes by a week. Folders 05 and 06 are the only two behind a confidentiality
agreement, and both are behind it for the counterparty's benefit as much as the
company's: institutional correspondence names people who did not consent to
publication.

## What each folder contains

### 01 Entity and ownership
Formation documents, the operating agreement, the SAM.gov entity record and
Unique Entity Identifier, the SBA Company Registry control number, and the
ownership statement. California limited liability company, formed October 2021,
single member.

### 02 The program
The Phase 1 protocol, the Phase 2 protocol, and the investigational new drug
application, each with its digital object identifier. All public.

### 03 The evidence
The six checkable quantities with their limitations, the ten-arm quantitative
systems pharmacology simulation with its verification notebooks, the
1000-patient digital twin, and the verification, validation and uncertainty
quantification exercise across 55 tests. All public.

### 04 Regulatory
The prepared investigational new drug application, the Pre-Request for
Designation letter, the parallel pre-IND and Q-Submission plans, and the FDA
correspondence log. **Nothing in this folder has been submitted**, and the folder
index says so on its first line.

### 05 Capital
The capitalization plan, the instrument comparison, the use of proceeds, the
Form D and state notice drafts, and the Treasury instructions. **No offering
exists and no instrument has been selected**, and the folder index says so on its
first line.

### 06 Institutional correspondence
Every letter sent to a federal mechanism, a clinical site, a foundation, or the
agent's developer, with its date and any reply. **No agreement of any kind
exists**, and the folder index says so on its first line.

### 07 Recognition and responses
The three presidential recognition letters and the favorable federal responses,
each described exactly as
[`brief-02-recognition-letters-use.md`](brief-02-recognition-letters-use.md)
specifies and never otherwise.

### 08 Team and roles
The current team, which is one person. The eleven-role site roster at 3.95
award-funded full-time equivalents, described as roles and not as hires. The
1.75 full-time-equivalent Phase I staffing plan.

### 09 Risks and stop conditions
The stop conditions, the four diligence questions that currently have no answer,
and the three costs of publishing the capitalization detail. This folder exists
because a data room without one is a data room a reviewer distrusts.

## The custody question

If the program stops, each artifact has a custodian. Regulatory records are
retained under 21 CFR 312.57 and 312.62 terms; the public deposits remain public
under their own licenses and require no custodian at all; entity and capital
records sit with the company and its counsel. Folder 09 carries the full custody
table.

## What is deliberately not in the data room

No participant data of any kind, because no participant exists. No institutional
document produced by an institution, because none has been produced. No drug
developer material, because no relationship exists. No robotic vendor material,
for the same reason. A data room containing a folder for something that does not
exist invites the question of why the folder is empty, so those folders are not
created.
