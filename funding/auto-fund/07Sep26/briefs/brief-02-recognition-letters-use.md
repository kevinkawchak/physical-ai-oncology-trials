# The Three Recognition Letters: What They Are, and What They Are Not

**ChemicalQDevice, San Diego.** Kevin Kawchak, CEO.
This brief fixes wording. It exists so that a true statement is made once,
precisely, rather than re-invented in each letter and drifting each time.

---

## The fact

Three presidential recognition letters have been issued to the chief executive of
ChemicalQDevice.

## What that is

A fact about correspondence. Someone in the executive branch acknowledged
correspondence from this company's chief executive, three times.

## What that is not

| It is not | Because |
|:--|:--|
| An award | No funds, no obligation, and no instrument accompany a recognition letter |
| A grant | The same reason |
| An endorsement of any document in this repository | No document in this repository has been reviewed by any office that issued a letter, and none is claimed to have been |
| A review of the science | No scientific review occurred |
| A commitment of any kind | Nothing is committed by either party |
| A federal relationship | Correspondence is not a relationship, and describing it as one would be a claim the record does not support |
| Evidence that any application will succeed | It bears on none of the criteria any mechanism applies |

## The one place they may be mentioned

**Data room folder 07, and nowhere else by default.** They are not mentioned in a
funding letter, a site letter, a foundation letter, or an investor letter unless
a recipient asks directly what federal correspondence exists, in which case the
answer is the paragraph below and nothing more.

## The approved wording, to be used verbatim

> Favorable responses to project applications have been received from federal
> funders. Three presidential recognition letters have been issued to the chief
> executive. Industry recipients of this company's communications have been
> responsive to its initiatives. Each of those is a fact about correspondence.
> None of them is an award, an agreement, a commitment, or a review of any
> document produced by this company, and none is presented as one.

That paragraph is already used in the site documentation package and in every
packet in this five-day block. It is reproduced here so that it is copied rather
than paraphrased.

## Why the discipline is worth the trouble

A reviewer who encounters an overstated claim stops evaluating the claim and
starts evaluating the claimant. This company's whole argument rests on the
proposition that its numbers carry their own limitations and its statements can
be checked. One loosely worded sentence about a presidential letter would cost
more than the sentence could ever gain, and it would cost it in exactly the
places where it matters most.

There is also a simpler reason. The letters are real, and describing something
real inaccurately is worse than not describing it at all.

## What to do if a recipient raises them first

Confirm the fact in the approved wording, and then move on to whatever the
recipient was actually asking about. Do not expand, do not offer to send a copy
unless asked, and do not treat the topic as an opening. A recognition letter is
the least interesting true thing about this company, and it should occupy the
least conversational space.
