\section{The Data Room}

A data room organized by document type is a data room nobody can navigate. This
one is organized by the question each folder answers, and every folder's first
line states what is **not** true of its contents rather than leaving a reviewer
to discover it.

\subsection{Custody, if the program stops}

Regulatory records are retained under the sponsor and investigator terms of 21
CFR 312.57 and 312.62 \cite{ecfr2026part312records}. The public deposits remain
public under their own licenses and require no custodian at all, which is one of
the quieter advantages of having published everything. Entity and capital records
sit with the company and its counsel.

\subsection{What is deliberately not in the data room}

No participant data of any kind, because no participant exists. No document
produced by an institution, because none has been produced. No developer material
and no robotic vendor material, because no relationship exists with either. A
data room carrying an empty folder invites the question of why the folder is
empty, so those folders are not created.
