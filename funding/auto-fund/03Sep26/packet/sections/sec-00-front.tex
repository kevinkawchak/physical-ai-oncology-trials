\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

A federal Small Business Innovation Research route would supply \$1,606,000 over
thirty-three months against a five-year program of \$3,500,000 in direct cost
\cite{capitalplan2026,tenapps2026}. The difference, \$2,104,000, is what this
packet is about. It sets out three candidate instruments at that size, the
conflict firewall each would have to leave intact, the seven lines the proceeds
would fund, and the one federal eligibility question that decides the choice
\cite{ecfr2026sbirownership}.

\textbf{This document is not an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to
buy any security. No offering exists, no instrument has been selected, and no
term sheet, simple agreement for future equity, or subscription agreement has
been signed.} Every instrument described here is under consideration, and every
size is a comparison size rather than an offered amount.

\section{What This Packet Is For, and What It Is Not}

This is the second of five daily packets in
\appfile{funding/auto-fund}. Day 1 asked five federal mechanisms a narrower
question than they had been asked before, because the approval of Rasonque on
August 26, 2026 removed a class of risk from the program \cite{fdarasonque2026}.
This day addresses the part of the program the federal route does not reach.

It also opens one commercial relationship. The agent's developer has never been
approached, and the letter in \appfile{funding/auto-fund/03Sep26/emails} is the
first approach of any kind. No drug supply agreement, letter of authorization, or
regulatory cross-reference is in place \cite{revmedexternal2026}.

\subsection{The line this packet does not cross}

Rule 506(b) of Regulation D permits no general solicitation at all
\cite{secformd2026}. A letter that reads as an offer to a person with whom the
issuer has no prior relationship is the exact thing the exemption does not
permit. So the outreach letters of this day ask for a screening conversation and
a read of the public repository, and they describe no security.

Three consequences run through every page that follows. No valuation, cap,
discount, minimum, or closing date appears anywhere, including in the figures. No
size is described as an offering amount. And the two filings prepared in
\appfile{funding/auto-fund/03Sep26/forms} are prepared and deliberately not
filed, because a Form D is due within fifteen days after a first sale and there
has been no sale.

\subsection{How to read this}

A reader with ten minutes should take \S3, which is the instrument comparison,
and \S4, which is the firewall the instrument has to leave intact. Those two
carry the decision.

A reader with twenty-five minutes should add \S1 for why the size is exactly
\$2,104,000, \S2 for the five letters and the order they are sent in, and \S5 for
what the company does with its own reserve on either branch of the decision. \S6
carries the positioning constraints, the method note, and the references.
