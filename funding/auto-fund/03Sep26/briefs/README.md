Three briefs in markdown for the readers who will actually price this: an
investor's analyst, an issuer's counsel, and a program officer testing whether
federal and private money are properly separated.

## Who these are for

Brief 1 goes to counsel and to an analyst. Brief 2 goes to counsel and to a
regulatory reviewer. Brief 3 goes to anyone who asks where the money goes, which
in practice is everyone.

None of the three is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any
security. Each carries that sentence in its own header, because a brief that is
forwarded without its covering letter has to carry its own disclaimer.

## The one number all three reconcile to

$2,104,000. It is the difference between the $3,500,000 five-year direct cost of
the program and the $1,396,000 of direct work a $1,606,000 SBIR route funds
inside its award. Every line of brief 3 sums into it, brief 1 sizes all three
instruments against it, and brief 2 asks what each instrument does to the
conflict boundary that protects it.

None of the three re-derives the number. It comes from the capitalization plan
and is used as given.
