## The one structural fact

The chief executive is chief executive of the sponsor and holds the whole of it.
He is **not** a clinical investigator on the trial this program describes, will
not be one, and is nowhere described as one. The investigator role sits wholly
with the clinical site.

That is not modesty. It is the only arrangement under which the financial
interest a founder necessarily has in his own company does not become an
investigator financial interest that a reviewer must weigh against the data.

## The four triggers, and why every one would fire

21 CFR 54.2 defines the disclosable interests. If the chief executive were also
an investigator, all four would fire at once.

| Trigger | 21 CFR 54.2 term | Would it fire | Why |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Compensation affected by outcome | Compensation whose value could be influenced by the study outcome | Yes | The founder's equity value is influenced by the study outcome by construction |
| Significant equity interest | Any equity in a non-publicly traded sponsor | Yes | 100 percent of a private company |
| Proprietary interest | Patent, trademark, copyright or licensing agreement | Yes | The company holds the workflow and documentation assets |
| Significant payments of other sorts | Payments to the investigator or the institution | Yes | The company funds the work |

Because all four would fire, the separation is not a matter of degree. It is
structural, and it is why the site holds the investigator role.

## What the site holds and what the sponsor holds

| Function | Sponsor | Site |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Protocol authorship and amendment | Yes | Reviews and concurs |
| Investigational new drug application holder | To be determined with counsel and the site | Possible alternative holder |
| Dose assignment decisions | No | Yes |
| Dose-limiting toxicity adjudication | No | Yes |
| Analysis plan authorship | Yes, pre-specified before first participant | Reviews |
| Analysis execution and unblinding | No | Yes, or an independent party |
| Investigator financial disclosure, Forms 3454 and 3455 | Collects and maintains | Provides |

A Phase 1 3+3 escalation carries no randomization, so the firewall binds on dose
assignment, dose-limiting toxicity adjudication, and the analysis plan instead.
The wording is carried forward deliberately so that it still binds when a Phase 2
successor introduces randomization.

## What each instrument does to the firewall

| Instrument | Effect on the firewall | Effect on disclosure |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Post-money SAFE | None until conversion. Holders take no board seat and no protective provisions at signing | Unchanged |
| Convertible note | None until conversion, with one exception: a maturity date can force a conversion at a moment not chosen by the company | Unchanged until conversion |
| Priced preferred seed | Board seats and protective provisions are usual, which introduces persons with governance rights and an economic interest in the outcome | Their interests may become disclosable if any of them has a role in the study |

The third row is the one to take to counsel. A board seat held by an entity with
an economic interest in the study outcome is not itself an investigator interest,
but it changes who is in the room when dose and analysis questions are discussed,
and the value of a firewall is that it is easy to describe.

## The open question this brief hands to counsel

Twelve questions from the partner research remain open and all of them bear on
the firewall. The most consequential four:

1. Will the developer hold or cross-reference the investigational new drug
   application, or will the sponsor hold it?
2. Can a site physician serve as sponsor-investigator, and would that be better
   or worse for the firewall than the current split?
3. Who performs safety reporting, monitoring, pharmacovigilance, drug
   accountability, data management and annual reports?
4. Who pays for insurance, indemnification, subject injury, and device and
   software validation?

The applicant, the site principal investigator, the FDA sponsor, the
investigational new drug application holder and the device manufacturer do not
have to be the same entity. Their responsibilities do have to be explicit, and
they are not yet.

## What is not claimed

No agreement of any kind exists with any clinical site. No drug supply agreement,
letter of authorization, or regulatory cross-reference is in place with the
agent's developer. No investigational new drug application has been accepted. The
disclosure obligations described above attach at a submission that has not
occurred.
