## What is never paid from federal award funds

The separation below is the same one the site documentation package sets out, and
it is repeated here because a private holder is entitled to know that their money
and federal money are not fungible in this company.

| Never paid from federal award funds | Paid from private capital or company funds |
|:--|:--|
| Lobbying of any kind | Yes, where lawful and disclosed |
| Fundraising costs, including any cost of this financing | Yes |
| Entertainment | Yes, and sparingly |
| Costs outside the approved award scope | Yes |
| Costs incurred before the award start date | Yes |

The rule is not a preference. Federal award funds are drawn under the award's own
terms, and the same activity can be lawful with one source of funds and unlawful
with another.

## The cost of publishing this

A company that publishes its capitalization plan gives up three things, and the
capitalization plan says so in its own risk section rather than hiding it. The
individual-scientist framing that made the original applications distinctive is
abandoned. Detail an owner might prefer private becomes public. And some prior
recipients will read the company framing as off-mechanism for the person-based
awards they administer.

That trade was made deliberately and is restated here so that a holder reading
the public repository knows the disclosure was a choice rather than an accident.

## What a holder should check before believing any of this

1. That the $3,500,000 and $700,000 figures appear in the ten-application work at
   the same values, unamended.
2. That the six quantitative results in the evidence pack each carry the
   limitation their own authors stated.
3. That no document in the repository describes any institution as a partner,
   sponsor, site, or endorser.
4. That the $36,330 virtual trial figure is described as **projected** wherever
   it appears, and never as estimated.

All four are checkable against the public repository without contacting this
company, which is the point of publishing it.
