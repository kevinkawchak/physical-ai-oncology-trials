## What this filing is, in one paragraph

California requires a notice for a limited offering made in reliance on the
Section 25102(f) exemption of the Corporate Securities Law. It is a state notice
that accompanies, rather than replaces, the federal Form D in
[`form-01-reg-d-506b-form-d.md`](form-01-reg-d-506b-form-d.md). A company
headquartered in San Diego selling to California residents will ordinarily have
both.

## The consistency rule between the two packs

Every field that appears in both packs must carry the same answer. In particular:

| Field | Pack 1 | Pack 2 | Must match |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Issuer name | Item 1 | Name of issuer | Character for character |
| Entity type and state | Item 1 | Entity type and state of organization | Exactly |
| Type of security | Item 10 | Type of security | Exactly |
| Total offering amount | Item 14 | Total offering amount | Exactly |
| Federal exemption | Item 6 | Federal exemption relied upon | Exactly |

A state notice that disagrees with a federal notice about the size or the
exemption is a discrepancy in two public records, and the discrepancy is easier
to create than to explain.

## After filing

| What happens | Note |
|:--|:--|
| The notice is recorded by the DFPI | Retain the confirmation with the company's securities records |
| Amendments | Required for changes the DFPI specifies; confirm the current requirement at filing |
| Other states | A purchaser resident outside California triggers that state's own notice requirement. Ask counsel before accepting a subscription from outside California |

## The three ways this goes wrong

1. **Filed on a different exemption than the federal filing claims.** See the
   consistency table above.
2. **A purchaser outside California accepted without checking that state's
   notice requirement.** The federal exemption does not preempt every state
   notice obligation.
3. **A fee schedule read from an old page.** Confirm the current fee on the DFPI
   site on the day of filing; a short payment delays the notice.
