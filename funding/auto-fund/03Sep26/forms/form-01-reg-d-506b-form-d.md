# Filing Pack 01: SEC Form D, Regulation D Rule 506(b)

**Portal.** EDGAR, [https://www.sec.gov/edgar](https://www.sec.gov/edgar).
**Rule.** [Regulation D Rule 506(b)](https://www.sec.gov/education/smallbusiness/exemptofferings/rule506b).
**Due.** Within fifteen days **after** the first sale of securities.
**Status on this day.** Prepared. **Not filed.** No offering exists and no
security has been sold.

---

## The prerequisite worth obtaining before an offering exists

An EDGAR filer identifier and access codes. The process requires a notarized or
otherwise authenticated application and takes days, not minutes. It is the single
item in this pack worth completing in advance, because the fifteen-day window
after a first sale is short and it is not extended by a company that had not yet
registered to file.

| Item | Where |
|:--|:--|
| EDGAR filer management and application | [https://www.sec.gov/edgar/filer-information](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/filer-information) |
| What is issued | Central Index Key, CIK confirmation code, password, and passphrase |
| Who holds them | The sole authorized person, stored where the company stores its formation documents |

## The four fields marked counsel only, and why

| Field | Why it is not the chief executive's to answer alone |
|:--|:--|
| 6, exemption claimed | The exemption determines whether the offering is lawful. It is the whole filing |
| 8, date of first sale | It starts the fifteen-day clock and is the fact the filing exists to report |
| 14, offering and sales amounts | An amount stated here binds the offering's public description |
| 15, investor count by accreditation | Rule 506(b) permits no general solicitation and limits non-accredited participation. This field is where a compliance failure becomes visible |

## After filing, once there is something to file

| What happens | Note |
|:--|:--|
| The notice becomes public on EDGAR immediately | Expect inbound contact from data aggregators and brokers within days |
| An amendment is required for material changes and annually for continuing offerings | Calendar the annual amendment on the filing date |
| Blue sky notice filings follow | See [`form-02-california-25102f-notice.md`](form-02-california-25102f-notice.md) for California |

## The three ways this goes wrong

1. **Filed before a first sale.** A public record of an offering that does not
   exist. This is the reason the pack is read and not filed today.
2. **No EDGAR identifier when the clock starts.** The fifteen-day window is not
   extended for an unregistered filer.
3. **A general solicitation that already happened.** If any outreach described a
   security or an offering term to a person with no prior relationship, the
   506(b) exemption may not be available. Every letter in
   [`../emails`](../emails) is written to avoid exactly this, and each carries a
   pre-send check for it.
