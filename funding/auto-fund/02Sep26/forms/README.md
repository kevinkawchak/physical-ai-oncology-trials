Two packs, each a field-by-field answer sheet for one online submission. The
chief executive opens the portal, opens the pack beside it, and types. No field
requires a decision that has not already been made in this repository.

## Why these two, and why first

`../../../potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/priority-steps.md` §13 records an NIH
warning that federal registrations can require six weeks or longer. A funding
letter that lands before its registrations do produces an invitation to apply
that the applicant cannot accept. So the two packs on the first day of the block
are registration packs, and every later day assumes they are done.

| # | Pack | Portal | Why it gates later days |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | [`form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md`](form-01-sam-gov-entity-validation.md) | SAM.gov, with Grants.gov and eRA Commons follow-through | An expired entity record blocks every federal submission, silently |
| 2 | [`form-02-sba-company-registry.md`](form-02-sba-company-registry.md) | SBA Company Registry | An SBIR application cannot be submitted without a current control number |

## What a pack contains

| Block | Contents |
|:--|:--|
| Portal and route | The exact URL, the sign-in method, and the office that answers when it fails |
| Prerequisites | What must be true before the first field is typed |
| Field table | Field label, the answer, and the character limit where the portal states one |
| Narrative fields | The full text to paste, already inside the stated limit |
| After submission | What is returned, how long it takes, and what to record |
| Failure modes | The three ways this specific submission goes wrong, and what to do |

## Character limits are checked, not assumed

Where a portal states a limit, the answer in the pack is written to fit it and
the character count is given beside the answer. A narrative that has to be cut in
the portal window is a narrative that gets cut badly.
