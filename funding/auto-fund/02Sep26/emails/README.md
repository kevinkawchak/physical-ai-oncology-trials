Seven letters in plain text, each complete: a `FROM` line, a `TO` line, a `CC`
line, a subject, an introduction, a body, a closing, an attachment manifest split
into what compiles from this repository and what the author adds by hand, and a
pre-send checklist.

Five are outbound re-contacts. **Two are replies to substantive responses that
came back**, and they are the more important pair: one of them corrects the shape
of the program's federal route rather than answering the question it was asked.

## Why there is no cold letter here

Nine of the ten application file sets in
[`../../../pdac-funding-applications/applications`](../../../pdac-funding-applications/applications)
were emailed between July 10 and August 8, 2026, and the tenth was addressed to a
clinical site rather than a funder. Every mechanism written to on this day
already holds an inquiry from this company. A re-contact after a material change
is a normal and welcome event for a program officer. A fresh introduction five
weeks later reads as though the first letter was forgotten, and invites the
answer that the first letter already received.

So each of the five opens by naming the earlier inquiry and its date, states the
one fact that changed, and asks a question the earlier letter could not have
asked. None of them re-explains the program from the beginning.

## The two replies received, and what they changed

Two of the five outbound letters produced substantive replies within the week.
Letters 6 and 7 answer them. Neither reply was a rejection and neither was an
encouragement; both were corrections, and the first is the most useful piece of
information this company has received from a federal office.

### The Cancer Therapy Evaluation Program reply

The Chief of the Investigational Drug Branch answered letter 3 by not answering
the question it asked. He corrected its premise instead, on two points that are
structural rather than negotiable:

| What the reply established | What it means here |
|:--|:--|
| Partnering with the program requires a cooperative research and development agreement, and that agreement is entered by the **drug company** | ChemicalQDevice is not the drug company. The agreement would be the agent developer's to enter, and it is not something this company can bring about |
| **All of the program's trials are investigator-initiated.** It does not sponsor studies a company provides to it | A concept originating with a company is the wrong shape by construction, however complete its documentation. If this idea has a route through that program at all, the concept belongs to an investigator at an institution |

The consequence is immediate and is recorded rather than softened. The site
conversation of day 3 stops being one step in a sequence and becomes the
**precondition** for the entire federal concept route. This company's role
narrows to the advisory software component and the documentation, which is a
smaller role than the one letter 3 described, and letter 6 concedes both points
plainly and asks for no exception to either.

### The Pioneer Award reply

The Program Leader of the High-Risk, High-Reward Research Program answered letter
4 with a route and a boundary.

| What the reply established | What it means here |
|:--|:--|
| The company may apply if its registrations are in place, or a partner institution may apply on the chief executive's behalf | Two viable routes rather than one, and the choice should be made on mechanics rather than on a guess |
| The chief executive would serve as Program Director or Principal Investigator, but an eligible institution or company must submit and **agree to house and support the research** | The operative clause is not the registration. Registering is a form; housing and supporting a clinical study is a commitment, and the same paperwork can make the two look alike |
| She offered to discuss the award and explicitly **not** the research | Letter 7 contains no sentence about the science, and its pre-send checklist requires deleting any that drifts there |

## What letters 6 and 7 do that letters 1 to 5 do not

| Property | Letters 1 to 5 | Letters 6 and 7 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Direction | Outbound, opening a thread | Replying inside an existing thread |
| Posture | Asking a question | Showing that the answer was understood before asking the next one |
| Attachments | The packet, plus deposits | The packet plus **one purpose-built one-page attachment each**, named in the file |
| The hardest sentence | The ask | The concession |

Each of the two carries a concession that costs something. Letter 6 accepts a
smaller role than the one it set out to claim. Letter 7 states the registration
status as it actually is rather than as it would read best. Both are cheaper
written now than discovered later by the recipient.

## The shared structure, and why every letter carries it

| Block | Purpose |
|:--|:--|
| `FROM`, `TO`, `CC` | So the letter can be pasted into a client without a lookup |
| `SUBJECT` | Written to be searchable by the recipient six weeks later |
| Introduction | Names the prior inquiry and its date in the first two sentences |
| Body | The changed fact, the ask, and at most two questions |
| Closing | A single offer of a short call, with no deadline attached to it |
| `ATTACHMENTS COMPILED FROM THIS DIRECTORY` | What the author compiles from this repository |
| `ATTACHMENTS THE AUTHOR MUST ADD BY HAND` | Deposited works, by digital object identifier |
| `EXTERNAL WORK TO CITE BUT NOT ATTACH` | Publisher PDFs, linked and never attached |
| `BEFORE SENDING` | The checks that are specific to that recipient |

## The four positioning corrections in every `BEFORE SENDING` block

Carried unchanged from
`../../../potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/priority-steps.md` §1, and extended by
a fourth that the approval makes necessary:

1. Daraxonrasib is not described as first in human anywhere. It is approved in
   the metastatic setting and was already in Phase 3 evaluation before that. The
   supportable claim concerns the integrated surgical and advisory workflow.
2. The robotic configuration is specified at the site agreement and not before.
   No manufacturer, model, arm count, or software version is asserted.
3. No institution is described as a partner, sponsor, site, or endorser. No
   agreement of any kind exists.
4. **New with the approval.** The approval covers the metastatic setting. It is
   nowhere described as covering the perioperative, resectable use this program
   proposes, and no letter implies that an investigational new drug application
   is no longer required.
