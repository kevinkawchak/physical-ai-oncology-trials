## The hold condition

This pack is submitted **after a site names a principal investigator**. A concept
form that names no investigator is returned by the trials office, and the return
costs two weeks and some credibility. The routing request in
[`../emails/email-01-ucsd-moores-escalation.txt`](../emails/email-01-ucsd-moores-escalation.txt)
is what produces the name.

## What is requested in writing alongside the pack

A preliminary written assessment covering staffing, pharmacy, pathology,
radiology, operating room research costs, specimen processing, data management,
monitoring, participant travel, insurance, and institutional overhead. Asking for
it in writing at intake is the difference between a budget conversation and a
budget.

## The three ways this goes wrong

1. **Submitted before an investigator is named.** The pack is returned. See the
   hold condition above.
2. **A drug supply route asserted that does not exist.** No agreement of any kind
   exists with the agent's developer. The field says so.
3. **A start date asserted.** Any date depends on a site agreement, an
   institutional review board approval, and an accepted investigational new drug
   application, none of which exists. The field says so.
