## The two conditions on submission

1. **An open cycle.** A letter of intent submitted outside a cycle is filed, not
   reviewed. Foundation cycles are published and are not negotiable.
2. **A yes on eligibility.** The letters of this day ask whether a mechanism can
   fund a Phase 1 whose sponsor is a for-profit small business with an academic
   clinical site under a separate agreement. If the answer is no, this pack is
   not submitted to that foundation, and the effort saved is real.

## Narrative field: specific aims

Paste the following. **718 characters with spaces**, inside a 750 limit.

> Aim 1. Establish, in up to 18 participants with resectable or
> borderline-resectable KRAS G12-mutated pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, the
> safety of perioperative daraxonrasib administered around a staged robotic
> pancreaticoduodenectomy, with 30-day device- and procedure-related serious
> adverse events as a co-primary endpoint.
>
> Aim 2. Determine the maximum tolerated dose or recommended Phase 2 dose in the
> perioperative setting through a 3+3 escalation.
>
> Aim 3. Demonstrate that an on-premises advisory language model can support the
> operative workflow with surgical authority retained at the interface, measured
> by completion of protocol-assigned robotic tasks without unsafe conversion.

## Narrative field: significance

Paste the following. **489 characters with spaces**, inside a 500 limit.

> Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma has the widest gap between incidence and
> survival of any major cancer in the United States. A RAS(ON) inhibitor approved
> in the metastatic setting in August 2026 has not been evaluated
> perioperatively in resectable disease, where a survival benefit would be most
> consequential. This study tests that setting with a governed advisory layer
> whose output never reaches an actuator.

## The three ways this goes wrong

1. **Submitted outside a cycle.** Filed, not reviewed.
2. **Submitted into a mechanism that cannot take a for-profit sponsor.** The
   eligibility letters of this day exist to prevent exactly this.
3. **A named site asserted before one exists.** The clinical site field says "to
   be named" and says that no agreement exists. A named site that has not agreed
   is the one error a foundation will remember.
