# Capital Instruction 03: The Site Start-Up Reserve, Held Short by Design

**ChemicalQDevice LLC.** Direction of the sole member and chief executive. Not
investment advice, not a recommendation to any other person, and not an offer or
solicitation. Nothing below has been committed or placed.

---

## What this pool is

| Item | Value |
|:--|:--|
| Allocation | $420,000 |
| Source | The site start-up and feasibility line of the private capital use of proceeds |
| Purpose | An institution's start-up work: coverage analysis, contracting, IRB submission, and first coordinator and regulatory time |
| Controlled by | A third party's invoice schedule, not by this company |
| **Committed to date** | **$0. No agreement of any kind exists with any institution** |

## Why it is not laddered

A ladder is the right shape for a pool whose draws are known in advance. This
pool's draws are not: their timing depends on when a site names a principal
investigator, when a budget questionnaire comes back, and when an institutional
review board meets. A ladder against unknown draws guarantees that a maturity
eventually has to be broken to pay an invoice, and breaking a maturity to pay an
invoice is the loss a ladder exists to prevent.

So this pool is held short and liquid, and the yield given up is the price of not
being forced.

## The holding

| Line | Instrument | Share | Order type |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Core | Government money market sweep | 70 pct | Sweep |
| Liquid | Short-duration U.S. Treasury ETF, one fund only | 20 pct | Limit, day, never market |
| Short rung | One U.S. Treasury bill, about 3 months | 10 pct | Non-competitive at auction |

The single short rung exists so that the pool is not entirely at a sweep rate,
and it is sized so that even if it is held to maturity, the sweep and the liquid
sleeve cover the largest plausible single draw.

## The four draw stages, and the trigger for each

| Stage | Draw | What it pays for | The trigger that releases it |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Up to $60,000 | Confidentiality agreement, contracting route determination, and the first coordinated start-up call | A written response from a clinical trial support service |
| 2 | Up to $110,000 | Budget questionnaire response, coverage analysis, and the institution's start-up fee | A named site principal investigator and a returned budget |
| 3 | Up to $150,000 | Institutional review board submission and the regulatory package assembly | An executed site agreement |
| 4 | Up to $100,000 | First coordinator and regulatory time before enrollment opens | Institutional review board approval |
| | $420,000 | | |

Each trigger is an artifact somebody else produces. That is deliberate: a stage
that releases on a date releases whether or not the work it funds is possible,
and a stage that releases on an artifact cannot.

## Reconciliation to the program budget

| Line | Value | Where it sits |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Program, per year, direct | $700,000 | The five-year frame, not re-derived |
| Site personnel inside that frame | Part of the $521,000 personnel line | The eleven-role roster |
| This pool | $420,000, one time | Private capital, not federal award funds |
| Overlap | None | Award funds pay award-scope work; this pool pays start-up work that precedes an award |

The last row is the one a program officer would check. Start-up work performed
before an award start date is not chargeable to that award, which is precisely
why it is funded privately here.

## The five checks before any of it is committed

1. **Does a written site feasibility outcome exist?** If not, nothing is
   committed. Enthusiasm is not a trigger.
2. **Is the institution named in a document the institution itself wrote?** A
   name in this company's own file is not an agreement.
3. **Is the draw inside its stage ceiling?** A stage that overruns is a signal
   about the site's real cost, not a rounding matter.
4. **Is any of this money federal award money?** It is not, and it must never be
   commingled with any that arrives.
5. **Is the liquid sleeve's limit price current?** Written on the morning of
   entry, never carried from a prior session.

## What this instruction does not do

It does not commit money to any institution. It does not assume a site. It does
not assume an award. It does not assume an offering, and none exists. It sizes
and holds a pool so that when a site does say yes, the answer to "how quickly can
you fund start-up" is a number rather than a conversation.
