unmodified, so a reader can check any claim in the La Jolla package against the
document it came from. Nothing in this directory is regenerated by the build.

## What the template supplies, and what it does not

| Carried forward from the San Francisco package | Replaced for La Jolla |
|:--|:--|
| The centered title block, the three stacked italic disclaimer blocks, and the table of contents that follows them | A ruled cover with a fifteen-cell document strip, an identity block, and a keyword line |
| One `\part` per document, with the part title reproduced in the table of contents | The same, with a `\clearpage` before every part so each document opens on its own page |
| `\S` for every codified reference, and enumerated subdivisions lettered `(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` | The same |
| Eleven documents: three state bills, three regulations, three building and premises codes, two operations documents | Fifteen documents: four legislation, three regulations, four building and premises, four operations |
| A 24-hour, 168-patient, 29-robot general oncology site in San Francisco | An extended-day, 18-participant, 14-robot Phase 1 pancreatic site in La Jolla, sized to one trial rather than to a service line |
| Simulation evidence as the sole quantitative basis | Simulation evidence plus a funded five-year budget, a staffed roster of eleven, and a conventional trial requirements manual |
