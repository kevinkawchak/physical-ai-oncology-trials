\section{Access Control and Physical Security}

\draftnote{Credentialing, badge classes, visitor escort, after-hours access, and
the retention of access logs. Align the log retention with document 03
\sect{}5.}

\section{Robot Operating Envelopes and Interlocks}

\draftnote{This is the section document 02 \sect{}5 points at, so the numbers
must be identical. Fix the envelope boundary, the interlock chain, the emergency
stop reach distance, and the recovery procedure after an emergency stop. Cite
IEC 80601-2-77 and ISO 13482.}

\section{Clean, Sterile, and Soiled Corridors}

\draftnote{Traffic patterns, the separation of clean from soiled, and the
handling of an instrument tray between the two. Keep the pattern consistent with
the air pressure relationships fixed in document 08 \sect{}3.}

\section{Waste Streams and Housekeeping}

\draftnote{Five streams: general, recyclable, regulated medical, cytotoxic and
pharmaceutical, and electronic. State the container, the storage location, the
pickup interval and the manifest for each. Keep the pickup windows identical to
document 05 \sect{}5.}

\section{Signage, Wayfinding, and Occupant Conduct}

\draftnote{Signage required at each zone boundary, the wayfinding path from the
parking structure to the clinical zone, which document 10 \sect{}3 also
describes, and the conduct rules that apply to occupants inside a robot
envelope.}
