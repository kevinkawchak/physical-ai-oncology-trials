\section{Why La Jolla, and Why Now}

This package stands up one clinical trial site in La Jolla, San Diego, for one
disease and one protocol. It is the successor to the eleven-document San
Francisco package, which described a general oncology site running on demand for
twenty-four hours a day \cite{sfsite2026}.

\draftnote{Open \mvfile{funding/move-in/inputs/READMES/README-Physical-AI-Oncology-Clinical-Trial-Site-Complete-Documentation-Package.md}
and take the eleven-document roster and the simulation evidence base. State in
two paragraphs what the La Jolla site keeps from it and what it drops, and why
the change is a narrowing rather than a reduction. The key driver named in the
master prompt, clause L, is the author's own body of work, the startup's funding
documents, and the White House and presidential support for independent
scientists; that driver is stated here and developed in document 15.}

\draftnote{Open \mvfile{funding/potential-partners/UC-San-Diego/README.md} for
